Captain cards

A captain card is a picture of one of your orcs that carries his whole look inside it. Drop one here to hold it up and see what's in it. The file stays on your computer: this page reads it in your browser and can't send it anywhere.

This page needs JavaScript to read a card. Nothing it reads leaves your browser.

    Move over the card to tilt it. Click it, or press Enter, to turn it over.

    Making a card

    In the game, right-click a captain on the Army screen to open the Captain Studio, then go to Quick > Cards. Move the picture frame over him and press Make the card. Open a pack rolls him several times and makes a card of each look, then gives him his own look back unless you keep the last one.

    Every card goes into your binder, which is the folder x64\sowframework\cards in your Shadow of War folder. To share one, send the PNG file itself. Open the exchange, on the same page, puts your cards up so other players can buy copies.

    Wearing a card

    1. Put the PNG in x64\sowframework\cards. Any file name works.
    2. Open the Captain Studio on the captain who should wear it, then Open the binder on the Cards page.
    3. Pick the card and press Wear this look on him. The ticks under the button choose which parts he takes: armour, head and hair, accessories, weapons, colours, effects, scars and paint, size and pose. What you leave unticked stays his own.
    4. His body never changes. Armour made for another body is swapped for the same design made for his, and anything with nothing for his body or class stays his own. An accessory the card doesn't have comes off him. Back puts his old look on again.

    The ticks, and taking off an accessory the card doesn't have, come with the next update. Until then, Wear this look puts the whole look on him.

    What a card carries

    The picture, the captain's name, class, body, tribe, level and traits as text, and his look as a list of the game's own record names. It doesn't carry your save, your Steam account or your exchange key, and a card from someone else can only change what a look you saved yourself could change.

    The card holds that data twice: once in the PNG file, and once hidden in the lowest bit of the picture's pixels, so it survives an app that strips a file's extra data. A screenshot of a card, a JPEG or a resized copy loses both.

    When a card won't open

    • It says JPEG, or not a PNG. The file was converted. Ask for the original PNG.
    • It says the card data is gone. The picture was edited or re-compressed with loss. The original file still works.
    • It opens here but not in the game, or the other way round: tell me on Discord and attach the file.

    Rarity

    Every card is a pull. Its tier, finish and power come from the card's id, by the same arithmetic in the game, on the exchange and on this page. Nobody picks them, editing a card's text doesn't change them, and the captain on the card doesn't matter: a plain captain can come out Mythic.
