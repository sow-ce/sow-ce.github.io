Captain scars and the return from death

The scar system as data and code: scar setups, types, variants and the parts they hand out, AddScar and the give path's rule that only slotless items pass their pairs on, what each part does to a captain's armour, scar decals and their layers, limb replacements and the sever hides, and how a return from death chooses its scar.

A scar is not a model: it is a set of items a scar type hands out, each painting decals on the head or the body, hiding part of the body or taking an armour slot. The engine gives scars through AddScar, titles, rewards, events and the return from death, and they live in the captain's item lists like any armour (see Captain appearance).

The scar data

A combat tree names its setup, and the setup lists the types it may carry:

Combat/Tree.ScarSetup -> Combat/Scar/Setup (Orc 22 entries, Olog 18)
  -> _StructuresCombatScarTypeStruct {
       Type            -> Combat/Scar/Type (24 records: WeightPerUse, ExcludedItems)
       ScarItems       the variants
       HelmetEquipment an item given with every variant }

WeightPerUse is the same list on every type, [1000, 10, 1, 0.01, 1e-4], indexed by how often the faction system has given that type (the scar-use table at FS+0x128, see The Nemesis system). Three types are bookkeeping with no items (ScarBookmark_Role_Claw, _Machine, _Stormbringer); HeadRemoved_Olog gives only the scar body; Resurrected_PlayerAbility_RaiseDead gives orc_Cursed / olog_Cursed, the look of Talion's raised dead.

A variant is an Inventory/Items record, and a part is one item it hands out: the variant itself when it carries something, and its PairedItems (hash 0xE512B887), recursively. Each part is one region of him. Its PassiveMaterialOverrides are Inventory/MaterialOverride records with a ModelPiece (0 body, 1 head, 13 body LOD) and MaterialParams primaries whose ParamNamePicker names the key: ScarLayer (a slice of scararray_d.texa, a float), ScarPosition (a UV, a float-pool pair in ParamValueVector2), ScarRotation (degrees), ScarScale (a pair), ScarTint, or SeverMask (a hide: a sever group, no decal). For example the orc burn orc_burns_WholeBody_Right (no builder, no decal of its own) hands out Body_Scar_BodyReplacement, orc_burns_body_rightArm (3 decals, layer 13), orc_burns_body_rightChest (4 decals on chest and back, and AD_empty_chest), orc_burns_body_rightLeg (1 decal) and orc_burns_head_massive_01 (5 face decals and AD_empty_head).

Giving a scar

AddScar

AddScar 0x7BB8C4(fm, type), 100 instructions:

  1. returns when FM+0x370 already holds the type;
  2. refuses when he holds any of the type's ExcludedItems (0xFCB040 over his items; 0x24DF7C on a spawned body);
  3. takes the setup from his combat tree's ScarSetup and gives one random entry of the type's ScarItems (0x7BBABC) and its HelmetEquipment (0x7BBAF4), each through the give path 0x355474 at source 10;
  4. only if one of those gives returned true: writes FM+0x370, re-dresses him from his items (0x61BAD4) and counts the use on the faction system (0x7BBA44), which lowers the type's weight for later picks.

It removes no older scar's items: scars stack, and FM+0x370 names only the latest. RemoveScar 0x1BC409C removes one random item of the type's list, so it can miss on a type with several. A member build gives only the role's ScarType; nothing in it reads FM+0x370, and its item clear removes the earlier scar items, so a rebuild drops a scar given by AddScar.

Pairs pass only through slotless items

The give path 0x355474 hands out PairedItems only for an item without an ItemBuilder. An item with a builder becomes an instance, is equipped (0x61B868) and the give returns at once (0x355600 -> 0x3556EC), never reaching the pair loop at 0x35568D. A builderless item is filed in the look-record list (FM+0x130) when it has a BodyModelChange or any override, then gives each pair recursively with the same source; its return value is whether it was filed, whatever its pairs did. Consequences:

  • Burns never registers. All four variants of each kind are bundles with no builder and no override of their own: they are filed nowhere and the give returns false although every pair landed, so AddScar writes no FM+0x370, re-dresses nothing and counts no use. In one save 0 of 116 scarred captains held Burns as their type, and one carried a Burns part.
  • A slotted variant's pairs never arrive. Poison's (orc_poison_head_0x: the scar body, orc_poison_body_rightArm, Tumor_Chest, IT_empty_shoulders), the orc Mauled heads' (Body_Scar_BodyReplacement and orc_multipleScars_body_01, 7 decals), Sack_Head's (HeadReplacement_DoNotScarHead) and Metal_Head's (orc_metalHead_head, the same decals the head carries itself). 0 of 3 Poison holders carried any pair; the control, 2 of 2 olog Mauled holders, whose heads are slotless, carried olog_cuts_body_leftBody and the scar body.
  • The olog Burns bundles exclude Trait_Olog themselves, so an olog never gets one through AddScar (their parts require Trait_Olog).
  • No slot-taking part has a slotless twin carrying the same override records (0 of 29).

Decal-only parts take no slot. The plain body draws no decals; its scar twin does (see Captain appearance, "Material overrides and decals"). So, by the items the give path really delivers: Poison, Branded_Slave and Mauled (and the olog Poison) bare the head; Burns bares the head and takes slots 0, 2 and 10 on an orc and 0, 2, 3 and 10 on an olog; Metal_Head and Sack_Head replace the head; MetalJaw uses slot 9; ExposedBrain, DaggerHead and AxeHead are helmets; cuts, eye wounds, Facepeele and Shamed are decals only; the claws use the accessory slot and the curse the body.

So the game ships one vein pattern as a decal, layer 0. The records place their decals by default, for example orc_Serpent_Eye_L at layer 26, UV (0.437, 0.854), scale 2; olog_spider_Head_LEye at layer 39, (0.59, 0.83), scale 1.43; and orc_Hide_Head_Eye_L is a hide, k_nSeverMask 4 (the left eye). The head and body shaders draw decal slots 0-7 only, filled lowest first; a captain whose own wounds hold those slots draws no further decal (one olog held 12 body decal entries, stitches in slots 0-3 and cuts in 4-11, and anything added landed at 12 and up).

Limbs and hides

Six designs replace a limb: AD_L_Arm_Claw, AD_BigClawL_Accessory, AD_R_Leg_Claw, AD_NazgulArm, AD_Blight_Arms (accessory slot 7) and AD_Zog_Legs (legs slot 5, one Slim piece for every body). Five of them share armour slot 7, so a captain holds one limb of those as an item. The orc's own limb is hidden by the item's PassiveMaterialOverrides, not by the piece: an Inventory/MaterialOverride names a model piece and a SeverMask over the sever groups:

Bit Group Bit Group
1 left lower leg 16 left upper arm
2 right lower leg 32 right forearm and hand
4 lower body (below the navel, pants) 64 right upper arm
8 left forearm and hand

The 46 shipped *_Hide_* records target model pieces 0 (body), 8 (shoulders: upper arms), 10 (arms: lower arms), 11 (pants), 12 (legs: lower legs) and 13 (body LOD); none spans an arm and a leg. On the three orc bodies' LOD0 meshes the groups are the lower legs (1, 2), the lower body below the navel (4), the forearms with the hands (8, 32) and the upper arms (16, 64); the chest (985, 982 and 1,180 vertices) carries no group, so no mask hides the chest. On gear the vertex alpha is the sever side of a limb part: shoulder pieces carry 16 on the left and 64 on the right, inside the masks of orc_Hide_Armor_Armu_L / _R (24 / 96), and the Easterling plate arms 8 / 32.

The claw and peg-leg items hide only the body skin and its LOD; the game ships armour hides that no item uses (orc_Hide_Armor_Arml_L, piece 10 mask 8; orc_Hide_Armor_Legl_R, piece 12 mask 2; the olog twins the same), so the limb's armour still draws over a claw. Two overrides on the same piece and key do not combine: only one store's value of a key is used. NazgulArm (orcs only) puts Orc_<body>_NazgulArm in the accessory slot (orc_buff_nazgularm.skel, orc_nazgularm.mat on gear_alphatest_lod1 with its own 512 x 512 atlas: pale skin, dark veins, rot at the hand and metal plates painted into the diffuse; its tint Metal_Leather_FactionCloth has no skin layer), with three hides (orc_Hide_Body_Armu_R mask 96, orc_Hide_Armor_Arml_R 32, orc_Hide_Armor_Armu_R) that cut the orc's own right arm and its armour away. It comes from Role_Accursed, Role_Hand and Role_StormBringer. The Blight arms (mask 120) come with IT_empty_arms (Role_Blight, PlagueBringer) and Blight_Shoulders.

Replace values: L_Arm_Claw 20, R_Leg_Claw 20, BigClawL 10, NazgulArm 0, Blight_Arms, IT_empty_arms, Blight_Shoulders and Zog_Legs 10. Of the 8 limb items only L_Arm_Claw (Trait_Berserkers, Trait_Defenders) and NazgulArm (Trait_Olog) carry an ExcludedCharacterType (the give path's reader 0x36686C skips an empty cell), and none a RequiredCharacterType. No Olog piece of the Nazgul arm or the Blight arms ships.

How a return chooses its scar

The cheat-death evaluator 0x1BC5D44 (166 instructions; see The Nemesis system, "Cheat death and the return") does, in order:

  1. for each sever effect on him, adds Combat/Sever/FX.ResurrectionExtraScarType to the list at FM+0x358 (5 records: a lost forearm gives LeftArm_ClawProsthetic, a lost leg RightLeg_ClawProsthetic);
  2. picks a scar through 0x1B5AA00(FS, [FM+0x370], cause, [FM+0x500]);
  3. drops that scar when his spawned body holds one of its ExcludedItems;
  4. with no scar and no extra: "Cannot resurrect -> No Available Scars", and nothing changes;
  5. otherwise increments FS+0x21C and FM+0x500, stores the scar at FM+0x348 unless it equals FM+0x370, and fires the cause's DeathEvent.

0x1B5AA00 (131 instructions) is the only reader of ScarType, SecondScarChance and SecondScarType. With no scar yet (FM+0x370 empty) it picks one of the cause's ScarType, weighted per type by use (0x1B5C97C). With a scar already, SecondScarChance[min(count, n - 1)] (0x7FD964, an unsigned min; count is FM+0x500 before its increment) decides whether one of SecondScarType (weighted) is picked; otherwise his existing type comes back and nothing new is added. Its compiled-out log line names it: "Selected Scar Type '%s', (was the existing scar type = %s)".

The data. Faction/Resurrection has 34 records, 33 of them causes with a scar field. SecondScarChance 0.5 with SecondScarType [Sack_Head, Metal_Head] is on 28 of them. The other five: the lost forearm and the lost leg (chance 1.0, no second type), Beheaded (no ScarType, chance 1.0, no second type), Cut in half (ScarBookmark_Role_Machine only) and Beheaded olog (HeadRemoved_Olog only). The first scar by cause:

Cause ScarType
fire, wraith fire, explosion Burns
caragor, ghul, troll Mauled or CutsHead
an orc's arrow EyeGouge or DaggerHead
Talion's arrow (and to the head) EyeGouge
an orc's slash MetalJaw, CutsHead, ExposedBrain, AxeHead
poisoned Poison
balefire Burns or Poison
flies, torment, curse Curse
a lost forearm or leg the claw or the peg leg, plus a role bookmark
beheaded (olog) HeadRemoved_Olog

Who else gives scars

  • Faction/Rewards/Definitions: 18 rewards with AddScarType, RemoveScarType, Included / ExcludedScarType (the pinnacle scars, the Hook and Claw role changes, Reward_KeepSlaveBrand).
  • Faction/Events/Events: 18 events (every *_Captured gives Branded_Slave, the three domination shames give Shamed).
  • Faction/Roles/Definitions: 6 titles carry a ScarType (GrogBurner Burns, FleshRot and its Nemesis Forge copy Poison, OneEye EyeGouge, Trainer and Grizzled CutsHead), applied through AddScar by the role setter and given again at every build.
  • Shadow of Mordor's scar types map onto War's through NemesisForge/FirebirdModelScarType.KrakenScarType: Wraith to Curse, Sack_Head to Sack_Head, Eyepatch to EyeGouge; Melee, Burn and Pin_Cusion have no War type (3 of 6 mapped). In Mordor a scar was a model in the appearance's scar slots (see Captain appearance, "The slots the game barely uses").

Open questions

  • What applies the scar a pending return stored at FM+0x348, and when? In one save 3 of 117 scarred captains held one, none equal to their FM+0x370; its consumer is not traced.
  • How often captains carry the items of more than one scar type in an unmodified campaign: AddScar stacks by code, but the one census taken (19 of 116) was not of an unmodified save.
  • Which of two hides on the same piece applies when a second limb item replaces the first: whether a replaced item's hide leaves with it is not measured.
