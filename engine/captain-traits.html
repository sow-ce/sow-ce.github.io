Captain traits, classes and personalities

How a captain's traits are stored, rolled and re-rolled: trait slots and their gate word, the resolver and the rebuild loop, the 64-trait cap, exclusive groups, what a save keeps, the traits the card never draws and who gives them, how elements are handed out, the trait card's rows, icons and text markup, and how class, advanced class and personality are represented.

Captains are FactionMember objects (see The Nemesis system). Their strengths, weaknesses, class bonus, advanced class, gangs, combat moves and quirks are all traits, held in trait slots and resolved by the engine from data.

Storage

FactionMember offset Meaning
+0x1E0 the trait slot vector {begin, end, u32 capacity at +0x10, u8 owned at +0x14}; a slot is 0x20 bytes (below)
+0x198 the active trait set, a vector of 8-byte Faction/Traits/Definitions pointers (it equals the set of slot traits in 528 of 531 captains)
+0x1B0 the marker vector (Marker_* records)
+0x378 a cache: the first active trait with GangLeader (0x1BC3CB4)
+0x508 derived bits: 14 any CanBypassRequiredDataLevelToSpawn, 19 any NPC summon / gang, 43 a trait that forbids sim events with a boss, 34 the slot list needs sorting, 38 the engine refuses trait edits (0x114B55C)

A slot is +0x00 the Faction/Traits/Picker, +0x08 the trait it resolved to (null = nothing), +0x10 the trait a story mission unlocked for it, +0x18 a u16 flag word, +0x1A an i16 option index (-1 = none). A picker's options are Picker.Traits -> _StructuresFactionTraitPickerEntry records (.Trait); an option entry is a primary record (its header byte +0x01 is 0x00, not 0x0A). There are 493 pickers, for 484 traits.

At most 64 active traits. Activation (0x3566B0) ends in 0x355114, which counts the active set and, above 64 (0x40) entries, deactivates traits picked at random from a candidate list (0x1BBA324) until 64 remain.

The flag word is the gate list and the re-roll mask

When a slot is added, its u16 at +0x18 is copied from the faction system's table FS+0xE0 (indexed by the picker's record index; 18,508 of 18,508 entries match). It serves two purposes at once:

  • the gate list - 0x1234E0(&slot+0x18, bit) (bt) decides which tests the resolver runs: bit 1 minimum level, 2 required row rank, 3 player follower, 4 required other traits, 6 intel level, 7 a campaign predicate, 8 difficulty;
  • the re-roll mask - the resolver 0x3528C4 first ANDs the rebuild's reason mask with it and, when the result is 0, returns slot+0x08 unchanged (0x352AB8: xor eax,eax; cmp word [rcx],ax; sete al). A slot is re-decided only by the events whose bit it carries.

Every picker carries bit 0 (493 of 493), so reason 0 re-rolls every slot. No live slot has a flag word of 0 (19,713 slots), so a zero word marks a slot that the engine will never re-roll: the resolver returns its trait for every reason.

The resolver returns the first option whose flagged gates all pass. Options with MissionUnlock_MinXPLevel set additionally require the level to reach the remapped MissionUnlock - 1 (0x352DB8) and the option's trait to equal slot+0x10 (0x3529A3); +0x10 is 0 on every live slot. Its option walk: 0x352BB8(&iterator, picker) over Picker.Traits (a 0x30-byte iterator: +0x28 index, +0x2C count), 0x352B2C the entry at the index, 0x352AC4 the entry's trait, 0x2774A8 "at end".

Adding, removing and rebuilding

  • UpdateTraits 0x3528A0(fm, reason) runs the rebuild 0xF88230:
mask = 1 << reason
if FM+0x508 bit 34: sort the slots (comparator 0x7127A8: Picker.SortPriority, higher first,
                    ties by address), clear bit 34
reason 0: active set emptied (FM+0x1A0 = FM+0x198), FM+0x378 = 0, FM+0x508 bits 14 and 19 cleared,
          byte FM+0x51C = 1
per slot: new = resolver(slot, &mask, &ctx)
          if new == old and reason != 0: skip
          mask |= bit 4              (a trait changed: later slots' required-trait gates re-run)
          deactivate old (0x353454), activate new (0x3566B0)
  • ApplyReward 0x3511F4 -> 0x351AF8 and five other callers rebuild with reason 0, so every reward re-rolls every slot.
  • Add = 0x7CD03C pushes {picker} (constructors 0x7CD150 / 0x7CD184, push 0x7CD0CC, growth 0x1144628), then UpdateTraits.
  • Remove = find the slot by picker, 0x353454 (drop it from the active set and re-derive only the bits that trait set; unregister its spawner hijack or catalyst; remove its items from a live body), then erase it (0x7A7378).
  • Activate 0x3566B0: gives the trait's items (0x355400 gives each Item through 0x355474 at source 8), registers a spawner hijack (0x1A32D88) for captains of the current region, and starts a catalyst only when he has a body. The give checks RequiredCharacterType against the type of his combat tree (FM+0x188; 0x30CF30, exclusion 0x36686C) before giving a weapon item (olog flamethrower or harpoon, the power-shot crossbow, grenades). Its first loop also writes the item's flair colors into the list at FM+0x118 ({u8, u32 channel at +4, ARGB at +8} via 0x1C10E54 / 0x1C11A74, seeded by FM+0x168).

What an away captain keeps. The give files an item only when it has an ItemBuilder, a BodyModelChange or PassiveMaterialOverrides (see Captain appearance), so a trait's plain items do not reach the record of a captain who is not in the world. Of 22 such captains with the curse weapon, 0 had CursedWeapon in their 25 item slots (FM+0x50) or their look-record list, and of 26 with the fire weapon, 0 had FlamingWeapon. The trait items kept away from the world are weapons, one per loot slot (19 items over 557 captains, source byte 8): crossbows in slot 2, and the Olog harpoon or flamethrower in slot 3. Two traits that want one slot therefore cannot both hold it. Gangs, bombs, mines, mounts and immunities are applied when the captain is built in the world.

Exclusive groups. 51 PickerGroup_* records cover 207 of the 493 pickers (Picker.ExclusiveGroup). 0x19F66C collects the groups of every slot a captain has, rolled or not, and 0x7DABFC refuses a picker that shares one. They are of three kinds:

  • design limits: DLC4_Ultimate (10 pickers), DLC4_Opener (3), BodySlamThroatGrab (2) and Mannerisms_Pool (23 audio mannerisms that never appear on the card);
  • one-of-a-kind features: GangOrPet (13), HasMount (2) and ElementalWeapon (5);
  • a strength paired with its own opposite: Resist_<element> (immune or resistant against vulnerable or mortal to one element), every <X>_Fear_Hate, BeastSlayerOrDismount, AggressiveUnAgressive, LastChanceModifiers, RepositionMoveAway, VaultBreaker, BodyguardDefeat, HateBossDefeat, KillOtherCaptain, WeakByHate_PinningShot, Alone_Fear and Blocks.

No rarity picker (Picker_OrcRarity_*, 3), advanced-class picker (Picker_BaseStrength_<advanced class>, 10) or class-bonus picker (Picker_BaseStrength_<class>, 6) carries a group, so the group test never refuses a second one: a member holding two advanced-class slots has both BaseStrength_* traits active (one such member read live).

The first option he qualifies for wins. Picker_GangOfDoppelgangers (flags 0x83: level and a campaign gate; group GangOrPet; excluded markers Marker_SpecialButterfly, Marker_Deranged) lists Quirk_HasGang_Doppelgangers_Elite (minimum level 35) before Quirk_HasGang_Doppelgangers (minimum level 15); both share the card name "Decoys". From level 35 that slot always rolls the Elite trait.

What a save keeps

A save keeps only the slots. The FactionMember loader 0x1BC159C (a keyed tree: Identity ... Inventory, Traits, PickerLevelRemap) clears FM+0x1E0 (0x1BB9068), re-adds each saved picker through 0x1BB255C -> 0x7CD03C with no trait, then calls UpdateTraits(fm, 0) unconditionally (0x1BC3554). Every load therefore re-rolls every slot: only single-option, level-only slots come back identical, and gated slots are decided again. The re-add copies the table's flag word back, so a slot whose word was cleared is rolled the game's way again. The loader bypasses the exclusive-group test, so a state that breaks a group persists. (0x1C12224 is the serializer of the online "searchable" index, not the save.)

Traits the card never draws

A trait is on the card when its UIGroup is one of the eight card rows' TraitGroups (below) and its CanDisplayInUI is not false (an absent cell is true). Of the 484 Faction/Traits/Definitions, 331 are on the card and 153 never are:

  • 143 have no UIGroup;
  • 3 have a group no row lists (UIGroup_MiscPassives for Quirk_Adaptable, UIGroup_OrcRarity_Epic / _Legendary);
  • 7 sit on a row's group with CanDisplayInUI false (Easterling_Deadly_Rage, _Lifesteal, _TargetsCaptains and _TripleShot on Classes, Easterling_Dismount_Rider on Bonuses, DEMO_ImmuneToCurse on Immunities, Resistant_GroundTakedown on the hint row).

Who gives them. Of the 153, 150 are an option of some picker (control: 327 of the 331 card traits). Combat moves have small pickers of their own (Picker_BodySlam, Picker_BlockCounter with Block_Counter_1 / _2, Picker_ThroatGrab, Picker_AudioMannerism_01_howl ...). No picker gives Marker_Is_Archer (named by Faction/Predicates/TraitLists) or Quirk_BloodBrother_AttackedByAI / _Killed (named by Faction/Traits/Relationships, the blood-brother bond). The 4 card traits no picker gives are the nemesis system's own: Hate_Nemesis, Terror_Nemesis, Hate_SuperiorDamage and Quirk_BloodBrother. Rarity is a requirement, not a source: OrcRarity_Epic / _Legendary sit in the RequiredTraits of the element and weapon pickers (Picker_Curse_Weapon, Picker_Fire_Weapon, Picker_FuryAttackCombo ...).

What they give. Most moves carry one ability item (BodySlam -> BodySlam, Block_Counter_1 -> ParentItem_CounterBlock1, Counter_Stun -> CounterStun); the five Terror_* give Item_Terror_General with OnAppliedItem Fear_LowHealth; Quirk_FalseRetreat has only an OnAppliedItem; the betrayal traits, Prevent_Resurrection, Quirk_Ambusher and Quirk_Sneaky carry no item. By their record names, the 153 are roughly:

  • 46 combat moves (counters, dodges, jump attacks, body slam, throat grab, whirlwind, grenades at his feet, dismount and their Easterling variants);
  • 34 behaviors (aggressive or cautious, ambusher, sneaky, false retreat, second wind, terror alone or at low health);
  • 11 company traits (elite gangs, summoned caragors, bodyguard counts, a DLC mount);
  • 36 story traits (betrayal, blood brothers, last words, no resurrection, self-raise, a DLC island's items);
  • 21 voice mannerisms (Mannerism_01 to _21);
  • 5 system traits (Marker_In_Hierarchy, Marker_Is_Archer, the three OrcRarity_*).

A trait's row is its record's UIGroup, not its kind: Quirk_OlogHarpoon is UIGroup_Classes (StrengthPriority 10, items Olog_2HR_Harpoon and Olog_Harpoon; it comes from the Tracker and Beast Master advanced classes), and Quirk_OlogFlameThrower is UIGroup_Bonuses (36).

How elements are handed out

The elemental weapons (curse, fire, poison, the necromancer's) are traits the reward system rolls; the game ships frost only for Talion and as captain defense (Immune_Frost, Resistant_Frost, Hate_Frost), with no frost-weapon record, picker, reward, hit effect or text. Picker_Curse_Weapon is held by 28 reward definitions through PickerGroupsToGive (EpicOrc_*, LT_*, KillPlayer_*), 22 titles (Faction/Roles/Definitions.PickerTraits) and 10 presets. Everything on that path is read live: the reward lists (Rewards/Lists.Reward), the weights (Definitions.WeightBase, the weighted pick 0x3505F4 under 0x350500), PickerGroupsToGive (0x351AF8) and the level table XPLevel.Entries (0x352DC4 <- 0x3513DC <- 0x34F9F0, which also calls 0x350500).

  • On a level-up the setups Default, Act1 and DLC3 name XPLevel::Default_StatusEffect: a chance of 0.1% at levels 5-10, 0.2% at 11-20, 0.9% at 21-35 and 1% at 36-60 to roll the list StatusEffect_StatusEffect (count 1, 15 entries, weight total 82), whose weapon entries are Curse 7.5, Necromancer 2.5, Fire 10 and Poison 10. A second table, Default_GiveStatusEffect (4 / 6 / 8 / 10% over the same levels), is named by no setup.
  • Exclusivity is markers. Each element gives Marker_StatusEffect_<X> and excludes MarkerList_StatusEffect_Not<X>, and Marker_NoStatusEffect sits in all three lists, so one element bars the others; MarkerList_StatusEffect_All lists every element.
  • The level tables also hold level-1 entries of chance 1.0 (Default_OrcRarity, Default_Resistances). Of the 1,239 reward definitions, 43 are held by no record.

Class, advanced class and personality

Class = FM+0x278 (a Character/Models record) + FM+0x188 (that model's own combat tree) + one class-bonus slot Picker_BaseStrength_<Infantry | Defender | Archer | Hunter | Savage | Olog>. Six pairs exist (Orc_Infantry, Orc_Defender, Orc_Archer, Orc_Spearman, Orc_Berserker, OlogHai_Infantry, each with its AI_<same> tree); the spearman's bonus is Hunter and the berserker's Savage (156 of 156 living row captains follow this). Changing the class re-rolls the body at the next build (see Captain appearance); whether a class is an olog's is its record's AISimulationConfig, not its name.

Advanced class = one slot Picker_BaseStrength_<Assassin | BeastMaster | Berserker | CombatMaster | Commander | Destroyer | Marksman | Tank | Tracker | Trickster> (155 of 156). All 16 BaseStrength pickers have table flags 1, one option and no exclusive group, so the slot's picker alone decides it, and it survives a save; the traits carry no item. AdvancedClass_* and OriginDeathTaunt_* are Faction/Rewards/Types weight records (InitialValue, OnUseThisReturnToValue, OnUseOtherValueDeltaScale) that no captain holds. A death-taunt "origin" is the title its reward gives (Reward_GruntDeathTauntOrigin_<X>: MustHaveAvailableRole RoleCategory_<n>_<X> with SetRoleFromFilter, the marker Marker_GaveOriginDeathTaunt and one level). The *Champion<X> rewards (CommonOrc_, EpicOrc_, LT_, KillPlayer_) are the Legendary promotion: Picker_OrcRarity_Legendary, the epic Picker_ET_Champion<X>, the BaseStrength slot, the advanced class's kit (for Destroyer_Fire: explodes on death, plants mines, explosive shot, grenaded retreat, grenades at his feet, fire weapon), the markers Marker_Specialization_<X> and Marker_JustEarnedAdvancedClass, SetRoleFromFilter and XPLevelDeltaMin 1.

Personality is the voice. FM+0x270 is a Faction/Personalities record (120 records; their ShortName is the voice set: pr, br, tw, wh, cv for orcs, ws, ze, bs for ologs, ea for Easterlings; empty for nonverbal and undead characters). Base tables live in Faction/PersonalityTable (11 orc, 3 olog, 4 mercenary). The engine's setter 0x1BC60A0(fm, record) (used by the reward SetPersonality 0x3523EB and by role changes that force a personality) removes the old personality's marker traits (0x79A1D4) and writes FM+0x270. It then re-registers a captain in the world with the new voice (0x73D548: CCharacter+0x2B28 and AudioHooks/List.CharacterSpawns on [char+0x2808]) and adds the new marker traits (0x7D3EA4), skipping markers while bit 38 is set. 0x7D3EA4 refuses a marker whose exclusive group is already held (every PersonalityMarker_* is in MarkerGroup_Personality), and the setter removes only the markers of the personality it replaces.

The trait card

The card's rows are Interface/Menu/MenuTreeNode/TraitGroup records, each listing its UI groups in .TraitGroups (TraitType 0 / 1 = strengths / weaknesses). The rows are Immunities; Hates; Class traits (Classes); Bonuses (Bonuses and Epics); Mortal weaknesses (MortalVulnerabilities and Terrors); Vulnerabilities; "Dazed by" (WeakenedBy and Fears); and a hint row of the resistances. A row shows at most MaxVisTraits traits (7; the hint row 3), a limit read only by the Details layer (4 readers), so it caps the card and not the captain. Miscellaneous passives and rarity are not drawn. The number under each row's icon is the captain's trait count in that row.

Icons are Icon strings (interface\textures\hud\traits\enemy\...\x.tex); base icons ship in interface\bundles\ingame_navmarkers.bndl. A trait's PreviewBundle holds only its preview movie, and the picture behind a card is the trait's own PreviewMovie. GameModes.Bundle gives each game mode one UI texture bundle: the fourth DLC's mode interface\bundles\ingame_tex_dlc.bndl (129 textures, 7.1 MB, all 42 icons of hud\traits\enemy\dlc4\), the third DLC's ingame_tex_dlc2.bndl (43 assets, no trait icon the campaign lacks), and the other 11 modes ingame_tex_talion.bndl. The campaign therefore never loads the DLC4 trait art: 41 of those icons (all but easterling_caragortamer.tex, which ships in the base set) are resident in no campaign bundle, and the card's request is registered with no data and draws a placeholder. The icons also ship as loose files, but a loose path alone stays absent: only a bundle makes them resident. The card builds from the path, so a reopened card shows art that has arrived since. The 484 traits use 161 distinct icons. Of the traits, 68 have no icon and 54 name pda\orcattributes\placeholder.tex (resident, a real picture); 116 of those 122 have no UIGroup. immunities\immune_curse.tex (only DEMO_ImmuneToCurse) ships in no bundle. The four DLC3 light traits share fx\characters\shared\combat\blade_light.tex, which the campaign's bundle carries. Monolith shipped identical pixels under several file names (damageboostenemyhealthlow = defenseboostselfhealthlow, damageboostselfhealthhigh = defenseboostselfhealthhigh), so the seven Easterling health traits use four images.

Text. The localization strings carry the card's markup (counts over the base game's 63,493 strings):

  • color runs {Color_<Alias>}text</font> (Enemy 593, Player 498, Objective 305, Broken 46, Intel 43, Epic 35, Follower 16, Reward 6, Weakened 5 ...); among trait names only Epic, which wraps 35 names whole ({Color_Epic}Epic Trait: Slayer Counter</font>); 36 of the 484 traits' Summary strings carry markup;
  • font runs <font face="$INTHUD">...</font> (51), glyph and button tokens ({Poison}, {CombatPrimary03}, {EpicSkull}), format arguments ({1!s}, {2!d}) and voice cues (<laugh>).

A color alias is a Profile/ActionAlias record (Color_Epic; GamepadString, KBMString, FontColor) whose FontColor links an Interface/Color record with an ARGB Color. The token pass ReplaceActionsWithDevices 0x2995F8 (named by its own assert) turns each {name} that is an action alias into a <font color> run of that color (0xD06CF0), after the game's own formatting. The aliases in use: Enemy 0xFFED5F09, Player 0xFF09D5ED, Objective 0xFFFFC640, Epic 0xFFE24E2C, Intel / Broken / Green 0xFF359737, Follower 0xFF09D5ED, Reward 0xFFED5F09, Weakened 0xFF94BFD6, Red 0xFFA42E06 and Hate 0xFFDF4E2C. Legendary 0xFF4700BF, Yellow 0xFFFFC640, Disabled 0xFF626262, Vulnerable 0xFF5279BD, Terrified 0xFF81294A and Immune 0xFFDC9B50 are defined but used by no string.

There are 21 card names that belong to two or three traits each; the record names' family words tell them apart (Easterling / East, D4RES / DLC, DEMO, Elite, Rare, Six, Archer, CMT for Combat Master, Perm, ET): "Decoys" / "Decoys (Elite)" / the DLC's six-decoy version, "Agile" and its Easterling twin, and so on.

Looks from traits. Of 334 addable traits, none sets ModelHide, BodyModelChange or AppearanceTags. Only Quirk_Undead changes the body and colors: Body_Scar_BodyReplacement -> IB_Body_Scar -> AD_Body_Scar.ModelPicker (the scarred orc and olog bodies) plus I_Am_Undead_Appearance(_OLOG) (flair colors, cataract-eye material overrides, material-set variants and the undead effect).

Open questions

  • What a slot with a zero flag word does across every trait type in play (gang, summon and spawner hijack traits on a captain who is in the world).
  • Traits gated on a marker (for example Raise_Dead, which wants Marker_StatusEffect_Curse) and whether they act when forced.
  • Does only one gang spawn for a captain holding two gang traits? FM+0x378 caches the first gang leader and 0x1BC3CB4's only caller is the deactivation; spawning such a captain and reading his company would settle it.
  • Which events give the four nemesis card traits (Hate_Nemesis, Terror_Nemesis, Hate_SuperiorDamage, Quirk_BloodBrother), and which code reads the item-less behavior traits (betrayal, Prevent_Resurrection, Quirk_Ambusher, Quirk_Sneaky) by name.
  • Whether promotions run the level-up reward path that hands out elements, and whether the engine's reward filter 0x105AEC(fm, reward) is where the element markers bar a second element: emulated against live memory on 18 captains it refused every element to each captain that already held one, but no rolled promotion has been observed.
  • How the Army stage draws an elemental weapon's glow for a captain whose trait items are not on his record: a shipped fire weapon draws there, and the route from the trait's buff tag to the stage is not traced.
