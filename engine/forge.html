SoW weapon mesh constraints (MEASURED 2026-10-27)

Reference: bundles\modelweapons\player\1h_sword\player_1hs_numenoreanswordt2.embb mesh 0 (player_numenoreanswordt1.mesh), AIRE model_open / model_view / model_export + OBJ slices.

  • Frame: grip point at the origin, blade +Y, width Z, thickness X (the flat faces +-X); bounds X +-4.35, Y -34.9..102.1, Z +-7.74 cm. Blender mapping (x, -z, y) / 100 is a proper rotation, so a Blender glTF export carries SoW axes (exported GLB bounds x 100 = SoW bounds, all 6 extents).
  • 810 vertices / 1,320 triangles, one bone "Twitch", stride-64 half4 vertices, cull distance 140 (N = 1 sword).
  • Blade carrier rings: 12 vertices (zero-thickness edge vertex, 0.28 cm steep micro-bevel at a constant absolute width, convex face, low centre ridge); rows every 10.55 cm, 6 rows for the point.
  • Blades are about 4x thicker than real swords: 9.23 x 2.30 cm at y 16, 5.16 x 1.23 at y 79 (1:4.2; a real longsword is about 1:12). Real-thickness blades read as paper in game.

Bake laws (MEASURED 2026-10-27)

Each cost one failed bake; full evidence in FORGE_GUIDE.md section 6. 1. Per-pair bakes into one atlas: margin 0, then one dilation from the exact coverage. The operator's margin paints over neighbouring parts' islands. 2. Nothing narrower than a texel may exist only in the sculpt source (a 0.3 mm edge land caught edge-pixel rays at 65 deg; dilation smeared them into dashes). 3. Fit the cage from the measured source-to-carrier deviation (BVH); a fixed 0.6 cm cage under a 0.70 cm deviation gave black holes (inner-surface hits). 319 -> 48 suspect pixels. 4. An island border is not an edge: no curvature wear within 2 px of a UV border. 5. Brushes on thin shells need backface masking. 6. Wear masks need broad curvature (blur r=4 + threshold) or hammer cells become rings. 7. Silhouette damage (edge nicks) must be in the carrier; a normal map cannot move an outline. 8. Procedural detail must be band-limited to the texel grid: point-sampled damascus put ~5.5 stripe cycles per 0.5 mm texel (noise); use the box-filtered square wave and game-scale layer counts (about 26, not a real 140). 9. AO is local (6 cm) and blind to studio props: the 10 m default reached the 20 m floor and gave an AO median of 0.094 (0.859 after the fix). 10. No hard rims in the sculpt: a wall ray runs parallel to a thin plate and grazes or misses a 90 deg rim. Sculpt plates get a half-round edge (armor.shell(rim=6)); carriers keep flat walls. 11. Averaged-normal cages (the Substance / Marmoset default) are wrong for thin shells: rays near a rim arrive at 45 deg and land on the wall (shoulder pair 433 -> 19,989 suspects). Default stays the operator's split-normal extrusion (--cage averaged kept for thick parts). 12. Ignore back faces: a tangent normal with z <= 0 is impossible; bake_pairs drops those texels and refills them from the island (curvature + position passes share the mask). The real gate is impossible_px_z_lt_0 in report.json; tilt 75-90 deg on rim borders is the rim seen edge-on. Pair: z < 0 texels 490 -> 10 (1,231 refilled); pauldron 0; sword 0.

Also measured: Blender stores BYTE-image pixels exactly as written whatever the colour-space tag, so sRGB must be encoded once by hand.

Handling probe: centre of percussion survives SoW-style blade thickening (about 55 cm in front of the cross on a construction-built sword; Albion's real XIIIa: 59 cm), the balance point does not (22 cm vs a real 10-15).
