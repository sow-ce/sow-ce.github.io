How Shadow of War works inside

This is what we have learned about Monolith's engine while building SoW:CE: the game database, the AI planner, combat, rendering, the world format, animation, scripting and more. Every entry says how sure we are, and most carry the exact code address or data record it rests on (Steam build 1.21).

Most of it was found with AIRE, our analysis tool. AIRE is not public yet; these pages are the knowledge it produced. Search below, or open a topic.
