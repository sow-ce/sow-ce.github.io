Items: inventory, gear, affixes, gems and skills

How items and gear work: the inventory inside the visual component, the item instance, affixes as the only source of gear stats and their level-indexed values, equip deltas on the player's stat table, gems and sockets, what a save keeps, the item lists combat requires, skill rows and the Skills screen, weapon looks and slots, and gear effects.

The inventory

The player's inventory lives in the visual component: vc = [[base+0x2797808]+0x2348] (vt_0x1FD63F0; the same object the Skills screen uses, [UIManager+0xB0]). The inventory proper is embedded at vc+0x3D8; 25 slot vectors sit at vc+0x4D0 + i * 0x18:

Slot Content
0 Melee (sword)
1 MeleeAux (dagger)
2 Ranged (bow)
3 RangedAux
6 Utility
7 Tribe
9 MeleeOffHand
11 / 12 / 22 cloak, armor, ring (by their contents)

The slot names are Combat/Predicates/InventorySlot. Index 0 of a slot vector is not the equipped item. The equipped items are named by the equip array vc+0x38 + slot * 0x28 (14 of 14 equipped items read). For the weapon slots 0, 1, 2, 3, 6 and 7, the entry is a holder (vt_0x1DB1F68) whose +0x140 is the item. For the cloak (11), armor (12), Isildur's ring (13), hands (14), scabbards (16, 17), hair (19) and ring (22), the entry is the item itself. An item is equipped when the array names it and its equipped byte +0xA9 is 1; the byte alone can lag a change.

  • Owned counts: vc+0x740 / +0x748, records {+0x00 item, +0x20 u32 count} (714 stacks on a finished save). Count 0x509074, consume 0x4F163C(vc, item, n), give 0x1BCE0C0(vc, item); the general give is 0x1C01E0(vc, item, n). vc+0x3D8+0xF0 is an append-only change log, not the counts.
  • Held test 0x24DF7C(vc, item): bit recordIndex(item) (0x1898E8) of the held bit set [vc+0x490] (0x24EB74); without a bit set it walks the stacks (0x36A17C). It has 102 callers (combat, quests, skills).

The item instance (vt_0x1FDABF8):

Offset Meaning
+0x20 u32 id
+0x24 u32 level
+0x2C u32 slot / holder index (copied from the equipped-weapon data by 0x1BF3750 / 0x1BF3474 / 0x649790)
+0x38 the Inventory/Items record
+0x40 the Inventory/EquippedWeaponData record (a weapon's look)
+0x48 the Inventory/ArmorData record (an armor's design)
+0x60 socket objects (a per-item path no shipped item uses)
+0x78 affix instances {+0x00 Affix/Definition, +0x08 f32 value, +0x0C f32 bonus from ModifyOtherAffix}
+0x88 / +0x8C the ability list's capacity / owned byte
+0xA8 / +0xA9 new / equipped
+0xB0 nemesis origin
+0xB8 the upgrade challenge (vt_0x1DC0F70; +0x78 progress / target, the card's "5 / 10")
+0xC8 GUID

The item destructor (0x1C7DB4 -> 0x114D828) always frees the ability objects and frees the list's buffer only when the owned byte is set. Captain traits that carry items are items of the same class.

  • Unique items. Items.MaxQuantity is 1 on 63 of the 340 records the player's gear uses; the engine refuses a second copy of such an item.
  • The nemesis origin (+0xB0) names a legendary after the captain it came from: a 0x20-byte block (allocated by 0x1BF1D74 -> 0x1C019F8) of +0x00 the Faction/Loot/NemesisGear record, +0x08 the Faction/Loot/NemesisGearDescription, +0x10 the captain's role (Faction/Roles/Definitions), +0x18 u32 the name hash (the P919 of an OrcNameGen1st_NNN key) and +0x1C u32 the rarity hash. The display name 0xD062C0(item, buffer, flag) formats the design's ItemName (from the look at +0x40, else the armor design at +0x48) with arguments from the origin; without an origin a legendary's key prints its placeholder ("Blood Sword ({1!ls})"). In one save 43 of 71 pieces carried an origin.
  • A look is read at equip. Writing an equipped armor's design (+0x48) in place does not redraw it, and the item can then no longer be selected; with the item off, the same write takes effect when it is equipped again. The in-place re-skin 0x645890 is meant for an item that is its slot's only one.
  • Removing a buff removes one instance. 0x6DEAC4 takes the first instance of that record in the combat object's buff list [combat+0x1A0] (0x45050C), then compacts the list (0x36B7A0).

Affixes are the stats

A weapon has no stats of its own: Inventory/WeaponData holds only sheath, effects and predicates. Every number on gear is an affix (Inventory/Affix/Definition, 2,961 records). An affix's value is FloatIndex.IndexedValues[ItemLevel - 1] of its VariableMin table (10 of 10 checked live). A sword's "Melee Attack" is the affix 1Sword_Base_Prop_<rarity or set> -> Float_DmgMod_M_FLAT (level 80: Common 160, Epic 166, Legendary 169); the player's own Float_DmgBase_M_BASE is 0, so the sword's roll is the whole flat input to the player's melee damage formula (DmgForm_Plyr_Melee).

Equipping adds deltas. 0x40136C(item, apply, owner combat object) calls 0xFE68F0 per affix -> 0x1A105D8(store, variable, +/- (value + bonus)). Unequipping subtracts the current values, so an affix value changed while the item is equipped leaves the stat table out of step. The engine's own upgrade (0xFBAAC0, at 0xFBB2EC: strip, level = min(owner level + 1, 80), recompute) removes, changes and re-applies. The validated equip 0x1BCA298 (equipping another item, or nothing, in the slot) removes every delta the engine's equip paths applied, and equipping the changed item the same way applies them again. Loading a save rebuilds the stat and chance tables from the worn gear.

The player's stat table is [[[player+0x26A8]+0x318]+0x60] + recordIndex * 0x50 = {variable record, +0x08 f32 value}, 622 entries (the Combat/Stat/Variable/Float category; lookup 0x1E53B8).

A level-indexed value table clamps an index past its end to its last entry (0x64AA40); the tables end at 91 to 94 entries.

Card text. An ability's line on the item card is its affix's Desc key, formatted with the value ({1!.0f}); the token {M} draws the Mirian icon. The 809 abilities a player item can carry print only 525 distinct lines: 70 lines repeat over 354 records. In 58 of those 70 every copy writes the same stat, buff or proc, one copy per rarity and set (sword base damage at level 80: 159 common, 163 rare, 166 epic, 169 on every legendary set); in the other 12 the copies differ. Gem lines are worded by the socket ("Weapon: ...% increased damage" is melee damage on a sword gem, stealth damage on a dagger gem and ranged damage on a bow gem). One shipped line is wrong: 4Shield_2Common_FollowerHPMod shows the sword's "{1} Melee Damage" text, while its only effect is VariableMod Mod_FollowerMaxHP. The abilities include 34 set bonuses (LegendarySet_<Set>_Affix1 / _Affix2, at 2 and 4 pieces) and 299 NemesisAffix rewards.

A save keeps identity, not numbers: id, level, the item, look and affix hashes, the GUID and the nemesis origin; computed values (such as 157.0 or 169.0) occur nowhere in the decrypted save. See Saves, the store and the network layer, for the inventory section's layout.

Gems

GemData.SocketAffixes gives one affix per socket type (red on a sword Float_DmgMod_M_PCT 0.10 - 0.30, green life steal 0.04 - 0.20, white Mirian 0.05 - 0.15, blade critical damage 0.20 - 1.00). Sockets belong to slots, not items. Six live socket objects (vt_0x1FDAED0) sit at [vc+0x3D8+0x80] = {entries, 8}, each entry {GemSocketType, socket*}; a socket holds +0x18 the gem, +0x20 the type and +0x28 its affix vector. Constructor 0x1BFB8AC(memory, type, gem), SocketGem 0x1BFBCB8, apply 0x1BFBC50(socket, 0 / 1, owner, GemItemLevel) over the socket's affix vector.

Items that combat requires

The player's combat accepts a held item only if the Combat/Predicates/Item lists name it: PC_Sword (73 items), PC_SwordOrDagger, PC_ActivateEquip (378), PC_Sword_Or_GroundGlaive, PC_SwordOrDaggerOrGroundGlaive and Trait_ItemBreaker_IgnoreSwordList. An item on none of these lists renders and equips, but no attack, combo or dodge starts with it. Items.WeaponType is null on every player sword and is not the gate for the player.

Skills

A skill row is an Inventory/ActiveItemGroup record (89): Entries[] -> _StructuresInventoryActiveItemGroupEntry {RequiredItem = the variant's unlock item (held = bought), ActiveItem[] = the *_SKILL / *_DUMMY items whose possession makes it active}. An unlock finds its row through Inventory/Items.ActiveItemGroup. These fields have exactly two readers:

  • The Skills screen's active test 0x1BCD6DC(inventory, unlock): unlock held -> its row -> the first entry with RequiredItem == unlock -> its ActiveItem[0] held. The node state 0x18A1770: 3 active (drawn white), 2 owned, 1 available, 0 locked; the effect pickers 0x19453D0 and 0x19480D4 ask the same test.
  • The click swap 0x1BC8C94(vc, unlock) (wrapper 0x1BC8C6C(vc, Skills record)): entries with another RequiredItem lose their active items, entries with this one gain theirs (inventory events at GM+0x6D18, types 8 and 7). Other callers: the loadout apply 0x1BCC1C4 (from the inventory re-init 0x4F0938), 0x17D9E4C and 0x1B56FA4.

Which move a button runs. The node runner 0x30FD80 tries the current node's links, then the root list of the tree at comp+0xEF0 in order; each root is resolved to its keyboard variant, validated (0x310C5C: type, cancel condition, transition; 0x310F10: the input) and PickAttack 0x275730 runs; the first root that yields an attack wins. An attack's priority (attack cache entry +0x0C) ranks attacks only inside one node. A skill variant whose moves sit in their own earlier root node therefore hides the later nodes on the same input: of 49 multi-variant rows, only Quick Throw does this (Rapid Throw, root 107, before the shared node, root 108).

A multi-projectile volley is one data link: AttackData.Multishoot -> Combat/Multishoot (one projectile per Entries wedge; FanOfKnives = 5), read at every shot by 0x2E8994 (0x6F6510; ProjectileCluster wins when set).

HUD button prompts: Combat/PromptOverride/Setup -> Override (one per button, CommandID) -> Prompts[]; the evaluator 0x277650 reads the compiled entry: +0xE8 IncludedItem (any of, 0x276E1C), +0xF0 IncludedItemAll (all of, 0x6E42A4), +0xF8 ExcludedItem. The link is copied at load (0x1C0DAE8); the list is walked live. _StructuresQuestPredicates.RequiredInventoryItemAll is the only other all-of item field.

Weapons and their looks

An orc's weapons are items in its inventory (FM+0x050): the held weapon at inventory+0x38 (item) and +0x40 (look), the thrown weapon through FM+0x060. The weapon's type is the item's Inventory/Items.WeaponType; the look (EquippedWeaponData) decides only the model.

  • The look's slot. EquippedWeaponData.ItemSlot is the inventory slot a look belongs to: 0 Melee (581 looks), 1 MeleeAux (407), 2 Ranged (697), 3 RangedAux (8: the olog arquebus, flamethrower and harpoon), 6 Utility (34: every Orc_Misc_* prop, drawn in the hand), 7 Tribe (15: exactly the Orc_Trb_* / Orc_TrbVis_* looks), out of 1,763. The game always pairs items and looks of the same slot (813 of 813 orc weapons in one census). A tribe weapon is drawn only while a tribal attack draws slot 7.
  • Where it is drawn and where it rests. EquippedWeaponData.Model -> _StructuresInventoryItemModel ActiveNode / InactiveNode: every weapon a class holds is drawn in RightHand and rests in a sheath socket (spears Socket_Sheath_Spr, archer crossbows Sheath_Archer, defenders Socket_Sheath_Def01, berserkers Ber_R_sheath, infantry and ologs L_sheath). Throwables are LeftHand with no rest socket: they exist only during the throw. Sauron's and the Nazgul's hand weapons rest nowhere.
  • Classes. Combat/InventorySetup::Orc_Spearman gives one weapon, Orc_1HR_ThrowingSpear, in the ranged slot: the spear is both the throw and the melee weapon, so spearmen have an empty hand slot. Berserker dual wielding is in the look: looks with an X2 suffix draw a model in each hand.
  • Every orc weapon look resolves the same way (EquippedWeaponData.Model -> _StructuresInventoryItemModel.Models -> _StructuresInventoryModel.ModelRecord -> a Model); see Characters for holders and how a drawn weapon is rebuilt.

Gear effects

A legendary gear effect is data wiring around an affix:

Inventory/Affix/Definition
  .Buff        -> Combat/Buffs                         a held effect
  .Proc        -> Combat/Buffs/Procs/Definitions (223) an on-event effect
  .Chance      -> Combat/Buffs/ChanceTypes             a bare tag (the key)
  .ChanceValue -> Combat/Stat/Variable/FloatIndex      "1" = 100%

Combat/Buffs/Procs/Definitions: Chance, Predicate, ResourceGain, Buff, MustBeDispatchSource,
  MustBeDispatchTarget, AllowDuplicateProcs, TargetBuff, TargetAppliedState, RandomStatusEffect
  Predicate  -> Quest/AutoObjectives    (the quest event vocabulary: PlayerHit_Sword, PlayerKill_AnyKill ...)
  TargetBuff -> Combat/Buffs .OneTimeAOE / .LoopingAOE / .OneTimeAOE_Player / .DeathAOE
                 -> Combat/AOE -> .Tick -> Combat/AOE/Tick (Radius, Angle, MaxAIToHit, Attack, AOEFX)

_Base_Prop_ affixes are always on (_SetProp_ affixes need the set's pieces). Across the 752 area ticks the radius ranges from 0 to 1,000,000 (median 550; 20,000 is the shipped "whole arena" value). Combat/AOE.Alignment decides who is damaged (*_DamageAll includes followers).

How an area ticks. Combat/AOE.Tick is a list of 1 to 8 Combat/AOE/Tick records (1 on 344 of 627 areas, 2 on 132, up to 8 on 7), fired in order: the tick loop 0x4E9D28 keeps the next one's index at +0x18C and stops after the last. It fires when the area's tick timer (+0x150, {clock, u32 end in ms}) has expired and re-arms it with TickDelay seconds (0x4EA90C: end = now + seconds x 1000); a timer without a clock counts as expired (0x4E9E98). The area's creation (0x15B994, "%s created AOE: %s") leaves the tick timer unarmed, so the first tick fires on the area's first update, and arms only the length timer +0x178, with MaxAOELength when that is above its threshold. Timer is an integer (1 on 583 areas, 2 on 41, 3 on 2, 4 on 1), not a duration.

Procs at run time

The affix apply 0xFE68F0 hands an affix's Proc to the register function 0x7A2AF0 on equip (0x7A2900 on unequip). Procs live in one global dispatcher, [base+0x26FFB70]+0xE8: 30 vectors of 0x18 bytes, one per DispatchPredicate.Type of the proc's trigger (0xE96DE0; a type of 30 or more is not registered). An entry is 0x48 bytes (built by 0x7A2A40 / 0x7A2A78):

Offset Meaning
+0x20 the owner (the player's combat object)
+0x28 the proc
+0x30 the proc's Chance key, copied at equip
+0x38 the proc's Predicate, copied at equip
+0x40 a count: a duplicate raises it unless AllowDuplicateProcs is set
+0x44 - +0x47 the dispatch source / target flags

Registration is skipped while [owner+0x30] is set. A later change to a proc's Chance or Predicate is therefore not seen until the item is equipped again, whereas its action fields (Buff, TargetBuff, TargetAppliedState, ResourceGain) are read each time it fires (0x714948).

Of the 223 shipped procs, 221 use a Predicate (215 of them from Quest/AutoObjectives, 6 from Combat/Buffs/Procs/Predicates), 131 a ResourceGain, 60 a Buff, 37 a Chance, 26 a TargetBuff, 10 a RandomStatusEffect list and 8 a TargetAppliedState, such as OnFire_MedDur. The resource a Combat/ResourceGain record restores is set by its ResourceType: 1 Focus, 2 elf-shot, 8 Might, 9 mount stamina and 10 Wrath, with no type meaning health. Nothing hands out 13 of the 223 procs, three of them complete (ProcDef_AoEFreeze_Execute, ProcDef_PoisonAoE_BrutalStealthKill and ProcDef_ConvertBeasts_WhileMounted). Combat/Buffs/ChanceTypes records have no fields.

Every firing adds a buff instance. A proc's Buff lands on the player as a new instance each time the proc fires, and its ActionsOnApply runs again. A buff whose StatDuration is empty never expires, so such instances accumulate. Shipped buffs without a duration include AoEFreeze, AOE_MoraleBomb_Small and PLBar_StunAoE_OnLowHealth. ConsumeWholeChargeMeter is true on 1,125 of the 1,126 Combat/Buffs records: it is a default and says nothing about a particular buff.

Summons. A buff summons through ActionsOnApply -> Combat/Actions/NPCSummon, as DLC4_SummonSpider_OnStealthKill does; otherwise the 50 summon actions are run by attacks (ActionsOnAttackStart_Source). A summon action names a Combat/NPCSummon record {AISpawnType, Count, SummonData, DeferredCooldown}, and its SummonData decides the side and the lifetime. With Alignment set to BuddyFollower or BuddyFollower_Beast, the summoned characters fight for the player; with MatchSummonerPlayerFollower, they take the summoner's side. A RemovalRecord (AmbShort_R, AmbLong_R) removes them; without one they stay. Of the 58 Combat/SummonData records, Hammerhand_Summon_Goblins summons 8 ghuls on the enemy side (Orc_FactionA) with no removal.

The game caps its own summoning buffs within the buff itself. DLC4_SummonGhul_OnStealthKill and _Spider_ last 1 s, summon on apply, and carry a one-time area (PLBar_Kill_ExcessSummonedGhuls / _Spiders). The area's first tick marks up to 4 ghuls or 6 spiders (MaxAIToHit) and its second kills the rest; both ticks hit only allies that carry Buddy_Summoned. A summon action's DeferredCooldown does not limit repeated firings.

On-hit chances

The player's chance table is [[combat+0x320]+0x228]: entries of 0x10 bytes {record, f32} and a u32 count at +0x230, indexed by Combat/Buffs/ChanceTypes record index and built on the first addition (flag store+0x240). A roll passes when the chance is greater than a uniform random number in [0, 1). The adder 0x1A2B314 builds the table when needed and adds; the subtracter 0x1A2B654 subtracts with no clamp and does not build the table, and its lookup asserts ("Bad record index") on a table not yet built or on a record of another category. A buff's chance modifier (_StructuresCombatBuffChanceMod {ChanceType, AdditiveReward}) is added when the buff is applied (0x4543C0) and subtracted when it is removed (0x454ABC).

Seven keys roll on the player's attacks with no ability at all: C_PC_ApplyFire (528 sword attacks, through PC_ApplyStatusEffect_Fire_WeaponKindRequired), C_PC_ApplyFreeze (529), C_PC_ApplyFireExecute (74), C_Machine_ExplodeOnGlaiveHit (40 held glaive swings), C_HeadExp (14 flurry kills), C_BrutalK_3xMorale (80) and C_Blast_HeadExp (80 chain attacks). The dagger and bow have their own: C_PC_ApplyFireDagger (158), C_PC_ApplyFreezeDagger (159), C_PC_ApplyFireHeadshot and C_ApplyFreeze_CriticalHitRanged (on 58 elemental-arrow attacks), and C_Celebrimbor_Headshot_FreezeAoE (on 10 heavy headshots). Keys such as C_BowCrit_HeadExp, C_PinOlogs, C_MarkExecute_HeadExp and C_Terror_Headshot_HeadExplode sit on bow item data and reach no attack without an ability of their own. Of the 78 keys, 11 have no holder in the shipped data.

The player's fire and frost categories carry no item gate (the orcs' gate on PredicateHasItem FlamingWeapon, CursedWeapon or PoisonedWeapon). Flame_Weapon is a chance bonus of +1.0 on C_PC_ApplyFire (Frost_Weapon the same on C_PC_ApplyFreeze). The sword's own poisoned and cursed states hang off a category nothing holds, so poison and curse on the sword need the buff.

When affix effects are read

An affix's Buff and Proc are read only when its item is equipped or a gem is socketed. The field bindings list three readers, all on those paths:

  • 0x40136C (log tag [COMBAT|BUFF]), called from the equip paths 0x4010D8 and 0x1BF2ED8, which reads the affix through 0x1BFBC50 and also applies the item's ActiveBuffData, ActiveVariableMod, ActiveMaterialOverrides and AppearanceTagsOnEquip;
  • the gem-socketing path 0x1BFBCB8;
  • 0x1C7FCD8, reached from the same equip path (0x4010D8 -> 0x1C68B58 -> 0x1C7FC04).

A change to an affix's Buff or Proc record therefore takes effect at the next equip, not at the next event.

Open questions

  • What the integer Combat/AOE.Timer controls (1 to 4; its relation to the length of the Tick list varies: equal on 337 areas, smaller on 271, larger on 19), and whether an area whose TickDelay is longer than its MaxAOELength gets past its first tick.
  • What the four records of the inventory's last slot entries are on a finished save (ids one above the highest item).
  • What reads Float_Temp_ForUnimplemented, the only variable the weapon life-steal and Mirian gems write (21 cell references against the 20 abilities that write it).
  • Whether code reads Equip_Has_1Sword_FireWeapon by name: Flame_Weapon's flag feeds it, and no record names it.
