Outposts, strongholds and fortress upgrades

How the game's outposts work across every layer: the engine's own name for them (strongholds); the per-outpost switch and the warchief missions it gates, from generation to the commander's walk-out; who can command an outpost; the alarm, captain spots and the HUD block; Lithlad's outposts as seats of an army, with their assault missions, retakes and boosts; the fortress-upgrade table every army keeps; the endless Shadow War's sieges; the spawn-scenario tables that fill a stronghold and the world scripts that drive them; and the world map that draws them.

Every region has outposts: warchief strongholds whose missions, map zones and signal fires each follow one saved switch. In Lithlad, five outposts are also seats of an army, with a commander and a reward. The engine's data and scripts mostly call them strongholds. The population inside one is shaped by spawn-scenario tables, and a fortress keeps a table of upgrades that its army's tribe and warchiefs choose.

The army, its seats and faction events are covered in The Nemesis system; quests and the campaign store in Quests, story and scenes; spawning in AI; and the menus in User interface.

Two names: outpost and stronghold

The game's text says "outpost"; most of the spawn data and the world scripts say "stronghold". Across the shipped database, 2,335 record names in 131 categories contain either word. The spawn tables are named almost entirely for strongholds:

The remaining names belong to the game around them: 1,000 or more audio and dialogue names, 174 inventory items (rewards, intel, the outpost boosts), 120 quest variable predicates, 88 sim events with their participants, 73 quest lists, 31 map canvases, 27 interface game-state predicates and 18 remote scripts (such as EnableAllOutposts and DisableAllOutposts). In the executable, 103 functions carry an outpost or stronghold name in their own strings or field registrations: 8 schema builders, 33 field registrars, 45 field bindings, 11 string users, 2 field accessors and 4 others. Among the others is the one script binding exposed, Faction::GetOutpostCount_PlayerFollowerOrEmpty (implementation 0xF647F0).

The outpost switch

Each region's warchiefs live at outposts: Lithlad DE_Outpost_Central / East / North / NorthWest / West, Núrnen I_Outpost_Harbor / Lighthouse / RiverRuins, Cirith Ungol MP_Outpost_Arena / LostHearth / Shelob, Seregost SM_Outpost_ArmyCamp / FrozenLake / Summit, Gorgoroth V_Outpost_BlackIron / Camp / Ledge, Minas Ithil MIOutpost_01..05. Each has one boolean in GameData/Variables, true by default and part of the saved state.

  • Its only writer is the world script method DeactivateOutpost in the regions' spawn-condition layers. A warchief mission's end paths call it (the warchief killed, one of seven domination events, or branded, but never a death threat), and only when the active quest is on that outpost's own list. Nothing in the shipped scripts sets a switch back to true. A count over the script copies the game loads (a later patch archive's copy replaces an earlier one; 1,403 of the 2,679 distinct script bodies) finds 193 bodies that name a switch, with 748 SetValue(false) calls, every one inside DeactivateOutpost (34 bodies, 22 switches each), and 0 SetValue(true) calls.
  • Its readers are the region's stronghold controller (below), which subscribes to the variable's change event, and 889 GetValue reads, every one of them in a side-activity layer's CheckMapCanvas (caravans, guarded caragors, camps, feasts, war councils, labor, supplies, drills); those run only while the outpost is active.
  • The zone on the map is Interface/MapCanvasSettings::<R>_ZoneMap_<X> (Núrnen 3, Cirith Ungol 3, Seregost 3, Gorgoroth 3, Minas Ithil 6, Lithlad 5). Its game-state predicate is the outpost's own switch (IS_Outpost_Harbor = I_Outpost_Harbor == true), so a zone is drawn while the outpost is active and dropped once it is cleared. Minas Ithil adds a story stage (100+ or 200+) or IsVendettaMode, and maps Common, Noble, TradeWest, TradeEast and Wild to MIOutpost_01 to 05; MI_ZoneMap_Keep has only the story stage. Lithlad adds NOT DLC4_OPCaptured_<X>. The zone's QuestPredicates only hide it while the outpost's own quest list runs. The mc* map-canvas objects placed at outposts belong to side-activity layers, not to these zones.
  • Where an outpost is. An outpost lies at the center of its commander spawn points, the AISpawnerAmbientQuestJob objects such as SC_Sp_<nn>.OutpostCmdr in the region's spawnconditions_op<n> layer. Of the placed objects, 116 carry "Outpost" in their name, every one a quest-job spawner, and the points of one outpost spread over 22 to 91 m. Seregost's Frozen Lake has no commander spawner: the 88 quest-job spawners within 120 m of its zone all belong to nemesis activities. The region's map information, however, names each outpost zone's canvas with a position (mcOP_ArmyCamp, mcOP_Summit, mcOP_FrozenLake); their x and z sit within 16 to 32 m of the spawn centers, while their heights are not the ground's.
  • Setting a switch back to true through the engine's own variable setter (0x59A1D8 on [GM+0x6C58]) relights the signal fires at once, through the controller's change handler, and the outpost's missions are offered again from a later world turn. Completion blocks nothing (see below), so the switch is the only lock.
  • In Desolation of Mordor's mode (DLC_DLC4Active) the same DeactivateOutpost captures instead of clearing: a win sets DLC4_OPCaptured_<X> true and the outpost's D_OP_* areas player-owned, and leaves DE_Outpost_<X> true.
  • OutpostsDisabled is not a feature switch: it is the data of the achievement OutpostDisabled, a count of the outposts the player cleared. Quest/Types::Outpost is unused: no quest has it as its type.

The warchief missions, end to end

The game's own outpost assault is built from data and the nemesis turn engine:

  1. Generation at a world turn. AdvanceTurn 0x1B5EE88 -> FactionSimEventGenerate 0x597A9C(army context) -> the candidate pass 0x597C7C over the seats -> per member 0x5924EC (his row FM+0x268, flag bit 27) -> the row's SimEventGenerationSetup entries in order -> a weighted pick 0x5926EC (per-type weights in the pass's own context at +0xA8 / +0xB0) -> 0x5935DC creates the event. The captain rows' setups (such as Island_Captain and SnowMtn_Captain) list the region's outpost types first (3 or 5 entries).
  2. The outpost type <R>_Outpost_OP<n> (Minas Ithil OutpostGeneric_OP<n>_NoAct1, Gorgoroth VO_, Núrnen IS_, Cirith Ungol MP_, Seregost SM_, Lithlad DE_) has the switch as its GlobalVarPredicate (in Lithlad, the switch AND NOT the capture flag; Minas Ithil's also wants StoryState 200 or more). It lists 7 events (Kill{Archers, Berserkers, Defenders, Ologs, Spearmen}_Outpost_0<n>, RapidStealth_, SustainedAlarm_), the high-priority ReleasePrisoners_UB_OP<n> (for a captain tagged UnfinishedBusiness_OP<n>), and weights [999, 0]: one event per outpost per army. Variant predicates such as DE_Outpost_01_Berserkers add a class unlock (BerserkersUnlocked).
  3. The event <Challenge>_Outpost_0<n> names the participant job SC_Subject_OutpostCommander, whose holder must be of row rank Captain, an enemy and alive, not on an event, not a bodyguard, spy, prisoner or vow holder, not an Easterling, with no revenge within 3 turns, not tagged OP<n>_UnfinishedBusiness, and not on a DLC outpost seat. The event also carries AddHenchmen, PreventHenchmenSelection, the builder OutpostCommander at chance 1.0, EventOnAssignSubject Outpost_Commander_Subject (one level more), the results SubjectFled, SubjectKilledByPlayer, SubjectKilled_Fallback, SubjectDominated, Shamed, Shunned, PlayerFledWarchief and SubjectSucceeded, Army-screen presentations, a portrait and AlwaysShowMemberNavMarkers. The commander holds the event at [FM+0x4F0] (+0x48 the sim event, +0x50 the quest record).
  4. The quest <R>_SC_OP<n>_<Challenge> (Seregost names its three SM_SC_W_, _P_ and _AC_) has the predicates {the event, the switch, NOT OP<n>_Retry running} and the type SpawnCondition: ActivationType 3, shown on the zone map, shown as new, kept on the HUD, replayable with XP and items on replay, startable in combat, and disabling travel points. Its objectives are {the challenge, SpawnCon_KillChief_Obj "Defeat {1!s}"}, with an extra objective picker Optional_Kills. It asks for player confirmation with the start screen QS_SingleParticipant_Base_WUIP, gives the rewards Outpost_<Region>0<n>Complete and Outpost_XP, has the outpost's zone as its MapCanvas and LimitGroup LG_OutpostMission, and uses the markers OutpostAlarm / CharacterOutpost. Each outpost has one quest list (for example DE_Outpost_01..05, IS_Outpost_01..03, MI_Outpost_01..05 and SM_Outpost_01..03), and there are also Outpost_Retry and a progress count per outpost.
  5. Placement. The event binds a placed quest whose predicates pass (0x597884 -> 0x19EFAB4, the map at QS+0x518; 0x19EFAB4 prefers the variant with the fewest completions), so a mission is offered only while its region is loaded. A placed quest is in state 2 (quest object +0x128), and a running one is Active, state 17.
  6. The commander comes out. While the challenge runs, the commander is not in the world. The layer script's OnObjectiveComplete waits for SC_ConditionObjectives, then a spawner set brings him and his gang (SC_Sp_<nn>.OutpostCmdr, SetOnScenarioSpawningComplete). He walks to a menace node while Player:QueuePresentation(WarchiefIntro_RL, him) plays his walk-out. When it finishes, his senses, traits and pathing come on, his henchmen follow him and SpawnCon_KillChief_Obj is displayed. A spy among his henchmen gets ActiveRevealSpyGuard, and the commander gets Warchief_Betrayed_IT (the betrayal branch of the same presentation). WarchiefIntro_EVT has five sequences. The default, WC_Default, asks of its target only that the attack category PC_DisallowShowdown is excluded. IsPlayerFollower is empty on every object predicate of all five branches, so the walk-out plays on any captain (as read from Núrnen's spawnconditionsop1 layer; the outpost missions of every region run the same spawnconditions* prefab).
  7. The end. The mission's scripts run DeactivateOutpost (setting the switch false) on its win paths, and the outcome's job events apply to the commander. Quest/Types::SpawnCondition is replayable (CanReplay, with XP and item awards on replay), and no predicate tests a completed quest, so completion blocks nothing.

Clearing an outpost turns its alarm off (<R>_Outpost_<n>_Comp, "You disabled the alarm in ... Outpost") and gives XP and an item. The XP is Combat/XP/EventQuest::Outpost_XP = FloatArray::Outpost_XP by player level (2,526 at level 1 to 66,306 at 99), times XP_LevelDiff. The item, Outpost_<Region>0<n>Complete (Faction/Loot/LootItemLists::OutpostProgress, ContributesToProgress, ShadowWarItem), is the Shadow War progress trophy and gives no stat bonus.

The card's text. Every outpost mission's DescriptionPicker is one of 11 Quest/JobStringPickerGroup::Outpost_* groups, all holding the same two entries on SC_Subject_OutpostCommander with PassIfNoMember true: IsNameKnown -> SC_<Challenge>_Outpost_Desc ("Draw out {1!s} by ...") and IsUnknown -> ..._Desc_Unk ("Draw out the Outpost leader by ..."). Both test MemberHasNameUnlocked = 0x1149E60(fm) = [FM+0x510] & 1. The quest's Description, ShortDescription and StartPointDescription point at one string, and missions of different outposts share pickers, so the text is per challenge, not per outpost. SpawnCon_KillChief_Obj is shared by 855 quests (outpost 233, vendetta 294, warchief and fort 100, other 228). A quest's text resolver is 0xCF9D90 (see User interface).

Outpost names. Each base outpost's AlarmAreas record names its switch in its HUD predicate, and its DisplayName is the key <tag>_Outpost_<n>. The mission's SC_<tag>_Outpost_<n>_Name and the capture line's <tag>_Outpost_<n>_Comp print the same name for the same n (17 of 17). The number n is not the outpost's order in its region: Seregost's Frozen Lake is 2 ("Oroshnalkh"), Summit 3 ("Gordumi Orlû") and Army Camp 1 ("Zunakh'mi Khaargh"); Minas Ithil's MIOutpost_01 is 2 ("Taghumi Ghor"), _02 5 ("Taghumi Omakh"), _03 3, _04 4 and _05 1 ("Shulgumi Shaghkam"). Seregost's quests number them OP1 Frozen Lake (SM_SC_W_*), OP2 Summit (SM_SC_P_*) and OP3 Army Camp (SM_SC_AC_*). As a data quirk, SM_SC_AC_RapidStealth names the sim event SustainedAlarm_Outpost_03 as its predicate.

Who can command an outpost

The commander is the captain the generator is working on, or nobody. 0x5935DC puts the generating captain in the cast list with the eligibility check on. The evaluation 0x61EF8C places him first (0x61FC88 -> 0x622170 -> 0x621834, a one-candidate run of the job matcher 0xF9BB20) and drops the whole event when he fails ("Fill Failed - Participant %s not able to assign to Job %s"). The job's builder is read only by the job filler 0x1BD40C4, and only when its seventh argument allows building. The evaluation calls the filler through 0x1BD2A54 with that argument 0, and lets a required slot stay empty when its job has a builder (0x7D7CB4 at 0x61F41B). No outpost commander is therefore ever built by a generation pass: the builder serves the quest side and the participant re-fill 0x5969A4.

The generator's own candidate tests. 0x597A9C returns at once while [ctx+0x1F8] is set. Otherwise it collects the seated members of every row (0x597C7C -> 0x597D7C) into two lists by FM+0x51D. It skips a follower with flag bits 27 and 28, a member already on an event (FM+0x4F0), a dead member not coming back (bit 2 without bit 4, 0x597E2C), flag bit 21, and a preset whose Faction/PresetMembers.DisableSimEvents is set. Before generating for a candidate, 0x597F28 skips flag bit 29, bit 43 when 0x1B5CFBC(FS, FM+0x2C8) says so, and a member whose body is loaded in the world ([FM+0x48] set with flag bit 1, unless an entry of the [FM+0x158] list, 0x10 bytes each, has its byte +0xE set). A captain standing near the player therefore gets no mission. SimEventGenerationType.ZoneMustBePlayerOwned is 0 (no requirement) on every outpost type and 1 (must be the player's) on NeverEndingShadowWar and DeathThreats_OwnZone; the value 2 (must not) is tested at 0x59365A.

Bodyguards are refused. A job's MustBeBodyguard is 0 (no check), 1 (must be) or 2 (must not), tested as FM+0x2D0 != -1 (0xF9F14B; FM+0x2D0 is the pool index of the member he guards, part of the boss key at FM+0x2C8). Late in a campaign most captains are a warchief's bodyguard (in one save, Gorgoroth 16 of 19 captain rows, Minas Ithil 16 of 19, Cirith Ungol 13 of 19). Only free enemy captains who guard nobody can command an outpost, so with 15 of 22 switches on, one outpost had its mission. A refused captain falls through to the Location filler (EvalChanceIfNoOtherExists). No event releases a warchief's bodyguard: Faction/Events/Events has only RemoveBodyguardToPlayer, its DLC4 twin and the BecomeBodyguard* family. Arriving in a region gives every free captain a SimEvent::Location filler (type 2, lifespan 3 turns, weight 5), so missions appear from the turn those fillers expire.

A pass without a turn. The script native System.FactionSimEventGenerate (0x1B82860) runs 0x597A9C on the current region's army; no shipped world script calls it. The engine's own [SIMEVENT] and [QUEST|JOB] log lines are compiled in behind byte switches (0x2A34DA0, 0x2A34DAF, in the block 0x2A34D98 to 0x2A34DB7), and the retail logger 0x19C83AC is a three-instruction stub, so the lines are produced and dropped.

No base-game event sends the player's captain against an outpost. Of 431 sim events, 163 let a required follower face a required enemy. Six of the generated events are attacks (Duel, CampRaid2, Raid_Caragors, Raid_Checkpoint, Raid_Supplies, Chase; a follower's win kills the enemy), and only the Duel moves a seat. No faction event and no sim event clears an outpost mission; only the quest's own DeactivateOutpost clears the switch. All 15 event results with an AreaOwnershipTransform are Lithlad's. None of the 50 outpost events (*_Outpost_0<n>, *_UB_OP<n>) carries a slot beyond the commander's (an ambush or reinforcement slot exists on 19 and 24 other events), and none of the 30 outpost layers holds a spawner for a second job.

The stronghold controller

One world-script class, strongholdcontrol, runs both the fortresses and the warchief outposts of the five base regions; its own comment reads "Set to a default value so that outposts don't do fort stuff!". Lithlad uses strongholdcontroldlc. The copy each region loads is the same 926-line body.

  • Start. A fortress calls FortStatusInit(<Region>Fort) once (4 regions). Each outpost's relay object (srO<X>Control, after a 1 s delay) calls OutpostStatusInit(<switch>), 17 in the base regions. In Lithlad each outpost runs FortStatusInit(DLC4_OPCaptured_<X>) and then OutpostStatusInit(DE_Outpost_<X>).
  • What the switch drives. The switch drives only the outpost's AINodeElevateTransientAlarm nodes (its signal fires). The map canvas calls are commented out ("handled entirely in the DB"): the zone follows its own game-state predicate. An outpost also stays dark while StoryState is below minStoryState (a per-placement property, 100 by default), a condition re-checked on every story-state change.
  • The alarm. Orcs that reach the signal fire raise the area's alarm. Above Low, the controller adds the HighAlarm spawn override list (training dummies, dummy archers and the arena categories off) and the red alarm canvases. At the second and third high waves it fires a fear burst: every tagged enemy flees, or 40% of them when a captain is present. A warchief mission raises the alarm to High when its commander spawns and resets it to Low when the objective ends.
  • An owner change in a base region swaps only the map style (Strongholds versus the Friendly_* canvases) and the gate logic. No spawner, override list or garrison changes there; garrisons that follow the owner exist only in Lithlad. On a loss the script writes the cave style to the main canvas variable (localMC) where the cave canvas variable (localCaveMC) is meant, in every loaded copy.
  • All outposts at once. The remote scripts DisableAllOutposts and EnableAllOutposts are fired 160 times in 101 loaded script bodies: story missions, DLC missions, tribe fights and statues silence every outpost when they start and release them at cleanup. The controller's enable path respects each outpost's switch.
  • Calls into the engine. The outpost scripts call AISpawnManager:AddScenarioOverrideRecord / RemoveScenarioOverrideRecord; Faction:GetAreaPlayerOwned, SetAreaPlayerOwned, GetOutpostCount_PlayerFollowerOrEmpty, VacateLocationList, CreateFactionMemberBuilder, AddFactionSimEventPicker, AddFactionEventJobSourceAndOther, IsEventOnEventList; System:ActivateRemoteScript, SetAlarmLevel, ElevateAlarmLevel, GetObjectsWithTag, IsDayTime; and the game-data variables' GetValue, SetValue and change events.
  • Class unlocks. The mission-variant predicates <R>_Outpost_0<n>_<Unit> are the outpost's switch AND <Unit>Unlocked AND NOT the capture flag. The same <Unit>Unlocked booleans gate the AI templates themselves (BerserkersUnlocked has 98 users, AI/Templates::Orc_Berserker among them): they decide which orc classes any garrison can contain.

The alarm, the HUD block and captain spots

The alarm level. System.SetAlarmLevel (script stub 0x1BA7EA4) is 0x1AA0A80(alarm area, level, flag); its only other caller is a quest's own AlarmLevel (0x4F86E8). It takes an AI/SpawnAmbient/AlarmAreas record and accepts only two levels of the area's TransientAlarmSettings: RequiredDefaultAlarmLevel (meter 0) and ElevatedAlarmLevel (meter = ElevatedAlarmLevelMeterMin, the elevated flag on). Any other level returns without a change. It writes the transient-alarm manager's entry for the area ([base+0x26FFBF0]: +0x40 level, +0x48 meter) and notifies the alarm system; 0x11FBC8(area) reads it back (the entry's level, else the area's default). All 22 outposts' areas use TransientAlarmSettings::Default: Low and High, meter minimum 300, falling 5 a second, so an elevated alarm falls back by itself in about a minute.

The block over the minimap. minimap.gfx's MM-Combat (Interface/HUD/Widget::Combat; the C++ widget vt_0x1F7CF88 at [[UIManager]+0x30]+0x48) has two parts: PlayerLevel (the player's own level, [[player+0x26A8]+0x568] + 1) and Alarm, the area's name, icon and cooldown. The area is the highest-priority alarm area brush around the player (0x60763C -> player+0x26E8 -> UIManager+0x1A8). The redraw 0x18D1974 runs when the area, the alarm level or its trend changes. With no trend, it asks the record's HUDPredicates (instance +0x10, through 0x1DDBE0), of which it is the only reader, and hides the alarm part when the predicate is false. The 17 base outposts' predicate is Interface/Predicates/GameState::<switch>, true while the enemy holds the outpost (Minas Ithil's also want StoryState 100 or 200); Lithlad's five, the fortresses and the Lumbermill have none. That is why Desolation of Mordor shows the name and alarm of an outpost the player holds, while the base game shows them only for an enemy's outpost.

Captain spots. Every base outpost has captain spots (AI/SpawnAmbient/Locations), listed by each region's captain rows: <R>_Outpost<X>_Tribal in Faction/Rows.Locations and <R>_Outpost_<X> in LocationsLowPriority (28 spots for the 17 base outposts; Black Iron and Minas Ithil's have no Tribal one). The location picker 0x78ADB8 is called from the location writer 0xFE3790 (the one writer of FM+0x330 and FM+0x338), from the fortress defense 0x1C1F244 and from the army's passes. It filters each spot through 0x592F9C -> 0x591D98(spot, the candidate's follower bit, ...): when a spot's MustBePlayerFollower link is set, its Combat/Predicates/Bool.Value must equal the candidate's follower bit, and then PlayerFollowerMustMatchOverlord applies (0 on every outpost spot). All 28 outpost spots name Bool::False, so they take enemy captains only, whoever holds the outpost. The game's own follower spots (the Overlord's and the follower war camps: 28 locations) name Bool::True.

Spawn scenarios and override lists

What stands in a stronghold is ambient spawning: spawn scenarios (AI/SpawnAmbient/SpawnScenario) grouped into categories (SpawnScenarioCategory) and filled through setups (SpawnScenarioSetup). An override list (AI/SpawnAmbient/SpawnScenarioOverrideList: Priority, Overrides, CategoryOverrides) changes them inside a ScenarioOverrideVolume or for as long as a script holds it (AISpawnManager:AddScenarioOverrideRecord). It names no unit types: it switches and caps scenarios and categories through two kinds of entry:

Entry (_Structures...) Fields
SpawnScenarioCategoryOverride CategoryType (a category), ForceEnabledStatus (a Combat/Predicates/Bool: True, False, or none to leave it), MaxConcurrentAI, MaxConcurrentSetup, Priority, MinDistance, AutoCooldownChance (-1 = leave as is)
SpawnScenarioOverride ScenarioType (one scenario), disable, MaxConcurrent, MaxConcurrentAI, FrequencyDecay, Priority

Three lists, as read from a running game and the shipped data:

List Priority Effect
Stronghold 4 categories Wilderness and AmbStartGraug off, AmbSentries capped at 3 bodies, AmbColumn at 4, SHOnlyEvent and FortPlayerWelcomeBlocker on; the scenario AIBlockout disabled (MaxConcurrent 0)
HighAlarm 8 StrongholdDummy, StrongholdDummyArcher and Arena off; StrongholdDynamic and StrongholdDynamicArcher listed with every field left as is
PlayerControlledSH_DLC4 1 Orcs off, StrongholdEasterling on: who guards a held Lithlad outpost

The tables never name a unit. A setup (SpawnScenarioSetup: ScenarioType, ScenarioCategories, RequiredAITypes, Priority, global and instance cooldowns, MaxConcurrent, MaxConcurrentAI, MinDistance, StartDisabled, ForceAISpots, ForceNextScenario, IgnoreMaxConcurrent, AllowFailedSpawn) belongs to a scenario (SpawnScenario: ScenarioCategories, Priority, FrequencyDecay, MaxConcurrent, MaxConcurrentAI, StartDisabled), and a category carries only MaxConcurrentAI. The shipped data has 151 categories, 53 scenarios and 282 setups, 87 of them stronghold or outpost setups. RequiredAITypes points at {AITemplate, NumRequired} entries: all 114 of them in the shipped data have no template and require 0 (a running game agrees). What spawns is decided by the spawners placed in each outpost's layers and the AI types they hold. Categories and override lists only decide which placed scenarios run and how many bodies they may field.

The code that applies them is one module of about 200 functions that reads every field of the override lists, both entry kinds and the setups. It is called from the ScenarioOverrideVolume class (vtable 0x1FB4BA0), a placed volume whose +0x120 holds its override list, and from the setup code.

How lists compete. 0x5ED360 resolves the overrides per running scenario: the spawn manager keeps its running scenarios in a table ([mgr], count [mgr+8], 0x60-byte entries with the scenario record at +8) and the active override lists in a vector at [mgr+0x10130] to [mgr+0x10138]. For each scenario it walks the lists and considers a list only when its Priority is strictly above the best one applied so far, so the highest priority wins and a tie goes to the earlier list. Inside the winning list, the direct Overrides entry for that scenario applies first, then the first CategoryOverrides entry whose category contains the scenario. A scenario no list mentions gets its own values back. The list priority is read in three places (0x5ED1B8, 0x5ED360, 0x5ED50C).

The stronghold vocabulary around them includes the distance predicates Stronghold_40M to Stronghold_150M (with and without line of sight), the despawn rules Stronghold_Removal50M to Stronghold_Removal150M and StrongholdCaptain, and the node tags Stronghold_Core, Stronghold_Leader, Stronghold_Gather, Outpost_Guard, Outpost_Captain and Outpost_Defense.

Lithlad's outposts: seats of an army

Desolation of Mordor's five Lithlad outposts are records of Faction/Outposts (6 records with DE_Fort; fields Row, Column, UIName, GlobalVarToSet, CreateSimEventOnDie, CreateSimEventOnVacate, PlayerOwnedItemGiverPicker). They are seats in an army's row table that a captain holds as the outpost's commander. The campaign's Lithlad is the same world (WorldInfo::desert), with these systems placed and switched off.

  • The capture flag. GlobalVarToSet (DLC4_OPCaptured_<X>) is written through the boolean setter 0x59A1D8, which fires the variable's change event. Its faction-side binding 0x100F4C0 has 5 callers: 0xFE2E90 (a seat change: true when the new holder is a player follower, false otherwise; true on vacate, then CreateSimEventOnVacate), 0xFD4DE0 and 0xFDCAB0 (bulk false), 0xFE0A60 (recompute) and 0x1010750 (a setter wrapper).   • The garrison follows the owner.   • Who has outposts. Faction/Definition.Outposts lists the five on 2 of the 38 armies, Faction_desert_DLC4 and Faction_desert_mercs_DLC4. The assault missions

Each Lithlad outpost has an assault mission (D4OA_C, _E, _N, _NW, _W; quest type DLC4_OutpostAssault, listed only by GameModes::DLC4, whose type table 0x598804 rebuilds; predicate DLC4Active), generated at a world turn while an enemy captain commands the outpost (DLC4_EnemyOutpostCommander_<X>). Starting one runs as follows:

  1. The start plays Presentation/Events::QuestScreen_Confirm_EVT, whose one element Quest_Confirm_FadeToBlack_ELM (camera hold, combat paused, slow motion, HUD cinematic, a fade action) takes the screen fader's lock 3: the screen goes black.
  2. The quest goes fade-out (4), loading (11 / 13), Active (17). The mission layer pD4OA_<X> runs QuestStart: the player is teleported to tpPlayerStart, then SpawnerSetup.
  3. SpawnerSetup asks the set pD4OA_<X>.setOwnCap (scenario setup 0_Missions) for 1 + N owner captains, N = Faction:GetOutpostCount_PlayerFollowerOrEmpty(Faction_desert_DLC4) (0xF647F0 -> 0xFD92F0), at most 4.
  4. Only the set's OnScenarioSpawningComplete queues the intro D4OA_Intro on the leader; the intro's fade-up releases lock 3, and its end runs StartAssault. The set spawns as one batch (5 bodies within 0.05 s).

The owner-captain slots are AISpawnerAmbientQuestJob objects (vt_0x1DC5360, 0x1E0 bytes, the job record at +0x1C0): slot 1 is PS_Subject (the sim event's subject, the outpost commander), slots 2 to 5 D4OA_Own_02 to _05. Slot 38 of the spawner (0x1AC21B8) takes its member from 0x1AC2288: the active quest (0x43D984; the container at QS+0x3D8) +0x1C8 -> {begin, end} of 0x38-byte job entries (+0x08 the job, +0x10 its alias, +0x20 / +0x28 the member key {FM+0x178, FM+0x174}). 0x1BD5544 matches the job (0x4FBE3C) and skips a member with flag bit 1, or with bit 2 unless the spawner's +0x1D1 allows it. Without a member the spawner does not spawn ("%s unable to spawn - FactionMember not found", compiled out). The setup's JobsToFill are D4OA_Own_02 to _05 (the subject comes from the event). The job filler 0x1BD40C4 skips the pool search when AlwaysUseBuilderRecord is 1 (0xF9A320), then rolls FactionMemberBuilderChance (0x1B3C6C; "%s Failed random roll"): a failure leaves the job empty, with no retry. With no member in a slot, the set never completes, no intro plays, and lock 3 stays held over an Active mission (a region change clears it). In the campaign the count reads Faction_desert_DLC4, which has no members there, and an empty seat counts as the player's, so every campaign assault asks for 1 + 4 owner captains, whatever the player holds.

The outposts' life is in the mission script (dlc4_outpostassault_*). FinalizeResults picks the sim event's result: SubjectDied when the mission is won with the commander dead; SubjectSuccess (his DLC4_Defended_Outpost event) when it is lost with him alive; and SubjectKilledButPlayerLost, with a new commander through CreateFactionMemberBuilder(DLC4_OutpostCommander_Understudy_<X>), when it is lost with him dead. The script binding Faction:CreateFactionMemberBuilder (0x1B7F544, 31 instructions) calls 0x1B5B824(FS, builder, faction 0, jobs 0): a null faction means the current region's army. IfVictory on a first capture sets DLC4_OPCapturedFirstTime_<X> and raises DLC4_NumberOfOutpostsLeveledUp, whose change adds all five DLC4_OutpostLeaderLevelUp_<X> pickers (three levels) to the faction. Their participant needs an empty seat, while their result targets the commander. Understudies are enemies of level 30 or more, seated by DLC4_PromoteUnderstudy_<X> -> DLC4_TakeOutpost. Every dispatched defender gets DLC4_Died_DefendingOutpost from the script's OwnerCaptainFactionEvent. The generation types DLC4_FakeOutpostDefense_<X> and OutpostCommanders are unused even in Desolation: no record links them.

  • A captain icon (0x18E68B4) takes kind 2 and his key, the widget from his marker type's target, and then LoadTarget 0x18EA410, which passes his states (0x18EA334 -> 0x10BD90) to the NamedOrc character data. A player follower is drawn blue 0xFF09D5ED, a default member in the enemy orange 0xFFED5F09, and an unknown one gray with no level.
  • Keys come from Profile/Commands and are dispatched by the layer's slot 12. MenuEnter (Space or Enter, the pad's A) runs 0x18E9AD4, behind the map's quest lock [layer+0xA30]. On an icon whose map target has AllowAsWaypoint off, it returns at once (0x18E9C88 -> 0x18EA300); on a captain icon it marks him as the target (0x18928A8; his HierarchyOrc type allows it); on free ground it sets a point waypoint (0x189286C -> 0x17A98AC([GM+0x6C80], &position, [layer+0xA10])). MenuAux06 (key 2) runs 0x18E97FC: on any hovered icon that is not a region, it tests only the key at +0x3A20, and when the key resolves and 0x1149E60 passes, it opens MenuManager.FactionMemberLayer, his captain screen with his 3D model. MenuAux08 (Home) cycles the icons, and Map (M) closes the map.
  • The clip and the states. The builder 0x18E6A40 and LoadTarget 0x18EA410 both ask whether the icon's marker type (0x18E73B4: for kind 6 the type its MapMarker rule picks; for kind 2 the member's own type, 0x4AA220) has CharacterData, and whether its target (the type's DefaultMapTarget, 0x18E7394) has InheritParentCharacterStates on. If both hold, the clip is the type's IconMovieClip, and LoadTarget runs the states (0xD88020) and puts the picture in the clip's child LoadTarget, or else in Content > LoadTarget. Otherwise the clip is the target's Symbol, sent to its normal label (clip slot 112), with the picture only at Content > LoadTarget. With no holder, nothing is loaded and the clip's own placeholder shows. The picture is a state's texture override, else the target's ZoneMapTexture, then its IconTexture, then the type's IconTexture. A map icon draws only a resident texture: IconTexture is a bare path that nothing loads, and only a card picture's DescriptionImgBundle loads a bundle. In sphud.gfx, SPH-UserWaypoint, SPH-SimpleMarker and SPH-CharacterMarkerCommon keep LoadTarget at the top with no Content; minimap.gfx's MM-LoadTarget (labels normal, new, blink) has Content > LoadTarget. Of 30 Interface/NavMarkerCharacterData records, ForgeTower tints its default state 0xFFED5F09 (state 6 SoP_Targeted 0xFFFFC640, 15 PlayerFollower 0xFF09D5ED), Collectible's default does not tint, and NamedOrc (42 types) runs a captain icon's 20 states. Of the 525 shipped Interface/NavMarkerTypes, 21 use Collectible, all on SPH-UserWaypoint with their target's flag on. An icon's states (0x18EA334) always include state 0; kind 6 adds state 6 while the map's current target is this marker, and kind 2 takes the member's own states.

The waypoint. The current target lives at [GM+0x6C80], with its type at +0x20 (7 a point, 1 a tracked target), its position at +0x10, its region at +0x28 and its marker at +0x90. 0x17A98AC(targets, &position, region record) sets a point and System::ClearCurrentTarget 0x17A8C5C clears it. The waypoint's marker (vtable 0x1DC0708) holds NavMarkerTypes::UserWaypoint at +0x38 and MapDisplay/Targets::UserWaypoint at +0xE0. The legend's outpost glyph is Interface/Menu/MenuTreeNode/Legend::Outposts (interface\textures\hud\glyph\outpost.tex).

The quest lock. [GM+0x6C80] is one cell for a quest's target, the Army screen's Mark Target and the map waypoint, and its setters test nothing (the shared clear-first core 0x17A8F6C and its 9 callers), so each screen asks first. The Army screen (0x321508) refuses when the active quest (0x103508: the first running, Active quest that stays on the HUD) has +0x2B0 set (no target) or requires one (0x624AC4: +0x2A8 not -1 with +0x2B0 clear). The world map (slot 29, 0x18E954C, run as the map opens) sets [layer+0xA30] to 0, and so refuses SPACEBAR, when the tracked quest record [UIManager+0xE0] has TargetForcedToNone or its quest object ([GM+0x6C60]+0x18, 0x40 bytes per record index, the quest at +0x28; 0x4FA590) requires a target. +0x2B0 alone holds nothing there, since it is copied from the field only when the quest starts (0x57407C). Of all quests, 60 set TargetForcedToNone, and on one of them the game itself answers SPACEBAR with "You cannot change your Target while on this mission".

Outposts on the map's own data. The world map lights outposts through material parameters. Interface/Menu/MenuLayer/WorldMap.OutpostStateParameters holds one entry, k_vOwned4567 (ownership) and k_vSelected4567 (selection), with four outpost slots of which two are bound (DE_Outpost_Central, DE_Outpost_West). The world map's update applies it (0xF252F0, together with the DLC4AreaMaterial region material) only when 0xD0B350 passes, which compares the current game mode against the name "DLC4": the lighting runs in Desolation of Mordor's own mode and never in the campaign. The zone map plays d4_menu_zonemap_outpost_over when the cursor is over an outpost (3DZoneMap.OutpostSFX) and steps an outpost's widget through stages of 2, 2 and 1 seconds (Interface/Shared.ZoneMapWidgetOutpostStageDurations). In that mode, a focused outpost ([layer+0xA90], written by 0x18E934C and by 0xD608B0, whose one caller 0xEF2F00 is one of the zone-map material's outpost bindings) gets its own card (0xDAECF0, which sets [layer+0xFAC0]). Key 2, with nothing hovered, opens MenuManager.MercenaryLayer on that outpost's Faction/Outposts.Row / Column through System::EnterFortAssaultCompleteFlow (0xD64800). Of the 103 map canvases, every outpost zone and the Stronghold_ZoneMap, Stronghold_Cave and Stronghold_Alarm1 to 3 canvases carry the enemy colors (zone fill 0xE6A41606, outline 0xFFA41606); Friendly_Strongholds and Friendly_Stronghold_Cave carry the friendly ones (0xE609D5ED, 0xFF09D5ED); and CP_Offense and CP_Defense are an orange and the friendly cyan.

The generic banner

The interface keeps a generic two-line banner that any text can fill. The script binding CPlayerObj::DEMO_ShowGenericBanner (a press-demo leftover, stub 0x1B80E20, two arguments: the player and a string key) is a jump to 0x1892F34(_, key, 0, 0). That function (37 bytes, two callers, first argument unused) takes the banner manager at [[UIManager+0x30]+0xE0] (nothing happens while UIManager+0x30 is null) and calls 0xE604C(manager, key, secondKey, flag). Each key goes through the string lookup 0x471F2C, the same table all game text uses, into a wide-character buffer, and 0xE7A98 (string "GenericAux") shows the banner: the first key's text as the title and, when a second key is given and its text is not empty, that text as the second line. The banner appears beside the minimap.
