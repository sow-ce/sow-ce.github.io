First Person Camera — Talion's own eyes, every camera (IN-GAME CONFIRMED 2026-10-07/08)

User: "the most cleanest first person camera ever"; after the two fixes below: "all working". Settings > Camera & FOV > First Person Camera, core/sow_first_person.hpp. ONE MinHook (user-approved 2026-10-07), created at boot, enabled only while first person is on; it replaced the old SetCameraPos 0x51C394 detour.

The camera pipeline (code + live):

owner = [[[base+0x26FFAA8]+0x6CB0]+0x6728 + i*8]   vt 0x1FD99F8; the local player's is the only
                                                  entry with +0xDEA8 set. +0x6720 is NOT a client
                                                  count (CORRECTED 2026-10-15): it reads 256 = the
                                                  per-character table size (259 live vt 0x1FD99F8
                                                  objects; same 256 as the sighting matrix)
owner+0xDEA8 -> CameraModifierData (md)
owner+0x5F0  -> modifier list (sentinel; +0x5F8 first node; node = mod+0x10, next = node+8;
                mod +0x08 owner, +0x20 its GADB record, +0x28 state, 2 = finished/skipped)
md+0x66C pos f32[3], +0x678 quat (x,y,z,w; forward = q*(0,0,1)), +0x690/+0x694 h/v FOV rad
md+0x650 / +0x688 = the PREVIOUS frame's transform / lens (0x520A60 copies at frame start)
  • Frame (0x2FC6B8): 0x51BF3C(md, dt) (uses the previous camera vs the focus), then loop 0x51BCA8: each modifier's vf7 edits md (validated after each), then the finalizer 0x51BE7C(md) stores the frame: md+0x66C -> md+0x768 output object (+0x3A4 transform, +0x3C0 start transform, +0x3F4/+0x3F8 lens).
  • The SCREEN reads that stored copy, not the live md: 0x2FEA2C -> 0x51AC00(owner, [GM+0x6CB0]+0x6FD0) -> 0x51AC30 copy -> 0x4E0CFC copies it to player+0x3420 and drives the view, camera transitions and lens from it (getter 0x115EF08). The live md+0x66C is the camera's own STATE for the next frame.
  • Gameplay = Camera/Behavior Camera_Primary: 12 modifiers (Adjustables, Lens, SetRoot, SmootherBegin, Distance, OrbitControl, AvoidObstacles, Pitch, Yaw, Roll, Smoother, Collide). Only CameraCollide (vt 0x1DB5FE0) calls SetCameraPos, which is why the old hook never saw kill-cams or finishers. Presentation behaviours are the *_BS/*_BR modifier prefixes.

The eyes (every index by hash, nothing hardcoded): a skeleton object's runtime skeleton [[[skel+0x10]+0x18]+0x10] holds +0x40 u32 bone count, +0x78 u32[count] P919 bone-name hashes in POSE order (Talion 128/128, face rig 241). Body pose [CChar+0xA0], face pose [[[CChar+0x22E0]+0x60]+0x60]+0xA0 (canonical head space). Eye = body Head re-rooted with the face rig's L_Eye/R_Eye midpoint: local = inv(q_faceHead) * (eyeMid - t_faceHead), world = CChar+0xAC + scale * R(CChar+0xB8) * (t_bodyHead + R(q_bodyHead) * local); face rotation q_char * q_bodyHead * inv(q_faceHead) (+Z forward, +Y up). Talion idle: eye 13 cm from Head, 77 cm above the character origin, face within 8.4 deg of his body facing. Hashes: Head 0x72598328, L_Eye 0xC22DABAF, R_Eye 0x34289675.

Who decides the direction: control behaviours (Camera*, Mount_*, Combat_*, Ranged_*, PlayAs_*, Default) = the engine's own orbit (mouse aim), rotation NOT written; any presentation behaviour or a dead player ([[CChar+0x26A8]+0x4A0]) = Talion's face rotation, with the gameplay lens held through the scene; Camera_Spectator (photo mode) or an engine camera > 30 m away (Army menu, map) = left to the engine.

Laws, each measured by a failure: - Never derive a rotation from the camera's own output. The 2026-08-11 "Level" mode read [player+0x2F90]+0x18C (the FINAL rotation, its own last write) and added a 6 deg trim every frame: pitch hit 90 deg in ~15 frames, then yaw flipped each frame (live: forward (-0.021, 1.000, -0.007)). - Write the eye, let the finalizer store it, then RESTORE the engine's pos/rot/lens. Leaving the eye in the live md made it the orbit's starting state: the orbit derives its direction from where the camera sits relative to Talion, so from inside his head the offset swung with every step - moving forward, aiming the bow and rolling spun the view (user report). Restored: fixed. - Create the MinHook at BOOT, enable on demand. A trampoline must sit within +-1 GB of the target (external/minhook/src/buffer.c MAX_MEMORY_RANGE 0x40000000 = 0x100000000-0x180000000 here); the game fills that range during play - five Enable clicks 24 min into a session all failed with MH_ERROR_MEMORY_ALLOC. FirstPersonCamera::Prime (InitializeFramework, after MH_Initialize) creates it; MH_CreateHook patches nothing and freezes nothing. Applies to ANY lazily-enabled hook.
