Comparison with Shadow of Mordor

How Shadow of Mordor (2014) differs from Shadow of War at the file and engine level: the executable, the archive format and its compression, container and asset format versions, the GameDB, the orc skeletons and animation files, the clip event language both share, Mordor's captain record, which of Mordor's orc meshes War kept, the Army screen's arena, and the Mordor-to-War nemesis importer that ships inside Shadow of War.

Shadow of Mordor (2014) is the predecessor engine generation. Its internal project name is Firebird; Shadow of War's is Kraken. The two games share their archive family, container idea, GameDB format and much of their orc rig, but nearly every asset format moved on by several versions, so no Mordor asset loads in War unchanged.

The executables

Shadow of Mordor Shadow of War
x64 executable ShadowOfMordor.exe, 27,930,624 bytes ShadowOfWar.exe, 117,732,784 bytes
Load address 0x140000000 preferred, relocatable (DYNAMIC_BASE, relocation table present) always 0x140000000 (relocations stripped)
Code a conventional .text section of about 20.6 MB .arch (about 30 MB) plus 70 MB of code in .bss a (see The executable)

Because Mordor's executable is relocatable, its addresses move with ASLR every run; the Steam build of War, which the War column describes, never moves (War's GOG and Microsoft Store executables are relocatable too; see Game builds).

Archives

Both games use LTAR archives. Mordor's are version 3 (.arch05), War's version 4 (.arch06, see Archives and file formats). The Mordor install has 66 archives, 45.4 GB.

header (0x18 bytes)  "LTAR" | u32 version (3) | u32 names size | u32 folder count | u32 file count | u32 1
names                null-separated strings from 0x18; the last string may run past the declared size,
                     and a small block precedes the entry table
entry (0x20 bytes)   u64 data offset | u64 compressed size | u64 uncompressed size | u32 flag (9) | u32 name offset

The entry table is found by its signature (the flag 9 at +0x18 of consecutive entries). Parsing all 66 archives gives 28,884 entries, equal to the 28,884 declared in the headers; every entry's data lies inside its file and the data offsets ascend in all 28,828 consecutive pairs. A name offset may point into the middle of a string, and an entry's name does not reliably describe its payload, so asset paths are taken from the containers' own path tables, as in War.

Compression. Mordor stores each entry as a stream of chunks {u32 compressed length, u32 uncompressed length (at most 65,536), data} compressed with zlib; chunks whose two lengths are equal are stored uncompressed (of 192 sampled chunk headers, 166 began with the zlib marker and 26 were stored). Chunks are padded, so the next chunk header can sit a few bytes after the previous chunk's data. War compresses with Oodle.

Containers and asset formats

Mordor's containers are BNDL version 4 (War: version 6); 8,034 of them hold 67,982 asset paths (66,731 distinct). Mordor's world prefabs are PRFB version 5 and its Scaleform movies GFX version 8 (ActionScript 2). Asset formats are older versions that War's loaders reject:

Format Shadow of Mordor Shadow of War
mesh (MMSH) 9 16-17
skeleton (SKEL) 8 (War's loader rejects it) 17
animation (ANIX) 42 44
texture (TEXR) 3 8
material instance (LTMI) 4 7-8
string table (SKDB) 9 (10 languages, 32,643 keys each) 9

Mordor's version-9 mesh vertex is 60 bytes: half-precision position (4 halves), 16-bit texture coordinates, 32-bit normal, tangent and bitangent, a colour, blend weights in BGRA order and bone indices; strides can differ between a mesh's elements. Every Mordor mesh has one UV set, while War's orc gear shaders read a second one. Mordor's texture coordinates can run to 2.0. Textures use the same DDS-based payload, and War's texture header adds a 23-byte block.

Materials are authored differently: Mordor's orcs use about 21 generic shaders, War's use the dedicated nemesis gear, head and body shader trees (see Rendering). For the one weapon compared directly (the Master Pike), Mordor's albedo was about half as bright as War's equivalent (mean 56 / 45 / 39 against 108 / 93 / 82).

The GameDB

Mordor's GameDB is a GADB version 17 file (the same container version as War's; see GameDB) of 23,264,671 bytes inside hotchunk.arch05's globaldatabase bundle: the entry after the named manifest entry, a BNDL v4 wrapping the database. It holds 115 character models named Cha_*. The Black Númenórean characters are three unique models: Cha_Belroth (the Hammer), Cha_Imrakhad (the Tower) and Cha_Zainzadan (the Black Hand, whose mesh one of Talion's alternative skins in Mordor is built from). Mordor ships no generic Black Númenórean soldier armour: a census of every container path (66,731 distinct), every self-named texture (12,714, none unreferenced) and every XML manifest (8,071) finds none.

The warband downloads (layer52 to layer56 archives) are 216-byte unlock stubs: the Guardian, Blood Hunter, Crusher and Flesh Burner orcs are GameDB presets assembling base-game pieces with paint, not new assets.

Orc skeletons

Shadow of Mordor Shadow of War
orc body rig 112 bones (the berserker 116) orc_base, 100 bones
shared bone names 72 72
face rig 33 joints 124 joints

The 72 shared bones sit at the same bind positions (median difference 0.07 cm, at most 0.94 cm for the core bones). Their bind rotations fall into three groups. 36 bind alike, within 2 degrees: the spine, legs, head and upper arms. 34 are re-posed: the hands and fingers hold another pose in the same bone frames, so a finger's child joint sits where Mordor's does in its own bone's frame to 0.01 degrees on 31 of 32 finger segments, and the weapon bone's local rotation agrees to 0.008 degrees (its grip 1.6 cm nearer the wrist). 2 are re-framed: the forearm twist bones, turned 81.46 degrees about their own long axis with the joint unmoved. The face rigs share no joint names. Of Mordor's orc pieces, those that use only bones orc_base also has: armour 35 of 40, flair 116 of 131, bodies 15 of 15, heads 44 of 111.

A Mordor version-8 skeleton has the same 37-byte bone records as War's version 17, but each record carries its own name offset; its part rows are 11 bytes (War's 14), its bounds a 24-byte box at 0x44 with no radius, and its strings are padded to 4 bytes (486 of 486 distinct skeletons read this way). The two games share one space: a Mordor orc piece holds its Head at (0.0, 72.76, 0.66) and War's orc_base at (0.0, 72.76, 0.67). Mordor stores its binds in model space as quaternions (x, y, z, w): converted to local rotations they equal the file's own bind clip on 111 of 111 bones (median 0.005 degrees), and every Mordor animation file carries such a bind clip, the rest pose as its clips see it. Mordor's humanoids name their bones as the orc rig does, so a file's rig is told by its bind clip's bone lengths, not by its names. War's own orc clips never track the ten twist helpers (L/R_ArmuX, _Bicep, _Forearm, _Wrist, _Thigh: 0 of 4,463 clips in 351 files): the engine places them.

Animations

Mordor's animation files are ANIX version 42 (War: 44). Mordor's loader (0xE44F80 in ShadowOfMordor.exe) reads a 0x1C-byte header (magic, 42, file size, the block chunk's offset and size, the animation chunk's offset and size) and then both chunks. The animation chunk holds a directory offset (+0x00), the clip count (+0x04), the node count (+0x0C), the channel count (+0x10), the string table offset (+0x14) and the node hashes (+0x18), then the channel hashes (position 0x00CE66FC, rotation 0x27436396, events 0x210C8F5A, the same as War's): a node by channel grid where version 44 has a run-length track table. A clip record is version 44's without the u32 pair at +0x74 / +0x78: its codecs start at +0x74, then one 3-byte entry per track {u8 channel, u8 node, u8 group}, aligned to 4, then the blockset table of 16-byte {offset, size, t0, t1} entries; version 44 has a byte group array and a u16 remap array instead.

The 6,656 files (22,390 clip records) use only codecs War also has: SV01 (6,655 files), SQ03 (6,655), AQ03 (5,392), UATR (482), STRI (436) and AV01 (237), with USBT, USFT and USCO in a few records. Every track entry is in range, and each codec sits on its own kind of channel in 2,076,293 of 2,076,294 entries. Mordor's executable registers ten more codec evaluators (CATR, CART, CSTR, CSRT, USTR, SQ01, AQ02, CACQ, CRS3, Q101) that no file uses. The dialogue clips are face motion: rotations on the face bones (brows, lids, eyes, cheeks, nose, lips, jaw, tongue) with the arms held still.

Clip events are the same command language in both games (KeyString hash 0xB9010BE0 in both; for example snd fol_cmn_attack_windup; BPW; STARTFACING; AFACE Angry;). Of 89,560 commands in 11,326 non-dialogue Mordor clips, 51,646 appear exactly (name and argument) in War's orc clips. Every gameplay command does (APPLYDAMAGE, AD, BCOL / ECOL, BHITWIN, BCANWIN, BTS / ETS, BPW / EPW, the facing commands, ComplexAnim, STOP_ALIGN), and so do the facial expressions (AFACE, RFACE, CLFACE). What War does not carry: Mordor's sound verbs (War's executable has no SND verb at all; see Audio and video), audio keys its database lacks, and the foot markers (HeelStrike, ToeStrike, HeelLift, ToeLift). Mordor registers its verbs as strings (AUDIO_HOOK_KEY, DIALOG_EVENT, SND, SND_UI, SND_GROUND, SND_HAND, the footstep verbs, BODY_IMPACT), where War compares hashes.

War ships Monolith's own ports of some Mordor clips. War's bip_cmbt_attack_front_1 (orcinf_cmbt_attack.anix) has the same 56 frames and 1,833 ms as Mordor's Bip_Cmbt_Attack_Front_1; retargeted onto War's rig (the hands re-posed as above), Mordor's clip matches the port to a median of 0.27 degrees per body bone, and the port adds a weapon grip in the fingers. Mordor keeps that grip apart: animation\shared\orc\orc_awareness.anix holds Orc_L_Hand_Weapon and Orc_R_Hand_Weapon, one static pose on 14 finger bones, while its body clips leave the hand open. War keeps the same two layers (orc_awareness_misc.anix) and plays the right one while fighting (41 starts in 250 s of one fight), and its clips carry finger poses as well (99 %).

The captain record (FactionMember)

Mordor's FactionMember is 0x3B0 bytes (its pool descriptor at 0x18022E8; War's is 0x528), in a pool array with a stride of 0x3B8. Addresses in this section are RVAs of ShadowOfMordor.exe. Fields, in the order the save writer (0x85BAE0) writes them where it does:

Offset Field
+0x48 u64 id
+0x50 u32 the name: the string-id hash of an OrcNameGen1st_%03d key
+0x54 pool index
+0x58 faction record
+0x60 combat tree, which is his class (AI_Archer, _Berserker, _Defender, _Infantry, _Spearman)
+0x68 preset member record
+0x70 / +0x78 / +0x80 traits: begin, end, u32 capacity; 8 bytes per entry (21 on one captain); the save writes 32 slots
+0x88 row record (0 = not seated)
+0x90 personality
+0x98 a Character/Models override (the preset's OverrideModel), read before any derived model
+0xA8 purpose
+0xB0 / +0xE0 / +0xF0 / +0x100 member keys: his own; his master's (the captain he guards); his nemesis's; the member whose nemesis he is
+0x110 the appearance object
+0x278 / +0x280 his bodyguards, 0x10 per entry
+0x290 / +0x298 the scar record (from his head's scar setup) and the brand scar record; a non-null brand means branded
+0x2A0 / +0x2A8 event history, {Faction/Events/Events record, u16 ...} entries of 0x10, at most 32
+0x2B8 / +0x2C0 role / location
+0x2D8 scar type (Model/Scar/Types), set by 0x84EE60, which also notifies the faction system
+0x2F0 NPC profile GUID
+0x340 level (the Army screen's power is level + 1, as in War)
+0x344 u32 level-up trait watermark: 0x8627F0 rolls the faction's LevelUpTraitSetup when the level passes it, then raises it
+0x374 u32 hash of one of his purpose's PurposeText lines (his "thought"), set by 0x856040 (33 of 33 matched)
+0x38C / +0x390 the "new information" bits and the time they were set, by the intel reveal 0x85F0F0
+0x398 u16 purpose expiry in faction ticks: FS+0x130 plus a random value from MinPurposeTimeOut (2) to MaxPurposeTimeOut (10)
+0x3A0 u8 seat column, 255 = not seated
+0x3A4 u32 status bits, default 0x01008000: bit 2 IsDead and bit 23 IsMockDead (the names Mordor's own captain document uses), bit 17 decapitated, bit 11 events pending, bit 7 set when he is shown as known on the Army screen
+0x3A8 u8 bodyguard list dirty: set on him and his master by 0x84EEF0, cleared when 0x857C40 recomputes the list
+0x3A9 u8 intel categories: bit 0 name known, bit 4 strengths and weaknesses known (stage 1 = 0x4F, stage 2 adds 0x30, 0x7F all)
+0x3AA u8 intel level: 2 full, 1 met, 0 unknown, 0xFF never raised; > 0 means known to the player

Orc content: what carried over to War

War moved every asset path (models\npc\orcs became characters\modelcharacters\orcs; 1 of 448 Mordor orc model paths exists in War) and renamed its records (0 of 202 preset captains share a name), so names cannot say which Mordor orc meshes War kept. Measured by surface distance instead (4,000 area-weighted samples per mesh, exact point-to-triangle distance, both directions, the 90th percentile in cm), each of Mordor's 419 orc meshes against War's 1,578 character meshes: "same" means within 0.25 cm both ways, "adapted" 1.0, "re-made" 2.0, and "merged" a Mordor piece lying inside a bigger War piece. Controls set those limits: different War designs of one slot are at least 3.58 cm apart for helmets, 4.28 for chests and 3.88 for one-hand weapons, while any two orc heads are within 2 cm of each other, so heads cannot be matched this way.

War kept, as the same mesh, the Beheader, Broken Shield, Bug, Metal Beard, Troll Tusk, Master and Juggernaut helms, the crown, spiked breastplates, face capes, the Butcher aprons (War's olog apron is Mordor's orc apron), two helmets and a set of weapons (crossbow, skull bow, javelin, kris, spiked and pick clubs, an elite sword, the saw sword, the shaman staff, throwing axe and spear parts, the huge hammer and the branding iron). Mordor's Black Hand sword is the exact mesh of War's nazgul_blackblade. Mordor only: all six class armour sets, the Bright Lord outfits, most hair and every head, the bodies and legs, the Black Captains' bodies, and 30 weapons (the Master Pike, five spears, axes, maces, swords and the orc bow among them).

At record level both databases are GADB version 17: Mordor 1,234 categories and 232,481 records, War 2,742 and 694,632. Orc look pieces 362 against 1,150, orc weapon records 158 (60 models) against 237 (97 models), heads (Model/SimpleModelHeads) 137 against 335, look classes 81 against 185, armour sets 28 against 17, colour setups 119 against 465, flairs 375 against 2 (War replaced flairs with items and tints), and scars 122 against War's 24 scar types (a different system).

The Army screen

Mordor's hierarchy screen is a world of its own, worlds\cinematics\wraith_visions\hierarchymenu_son.bndl (son.arch05 entry 15, 232,667,989 bytes, 446 assets; near-identical copies in intro.arch05 and outro.arch05): a scene, an object database, a world mesh and 10 prefabs (the hierarchy art, the Warchief pedestals, glyphs, orc light posts, a ruin wall, backdrop rocks), with the crowd drawn as card meshes (fx\ui\hierarchy\models\orcarmy_outlinecards.mesh). The seats are logical rows and columns in _StructuresFaction.HierarchyFactionPos (5 Warchiefs and captain rows of 8, 7 and 5: 25 seats), placed in the world as objects Hier.Faction_<row>_<column> with a character spot .Chr, a camera .Cam and about 20 points of interest each. The screen's movie is interface\flash\widgets\hierarchy.gfx, which holds no logic.

The Mordor importer in Shadow of War

Shadow of War ships its own importer for Shadow of Mordor captains, the Nemesis Forge: it rebuilds a player's Mordor follower and nemesis as War captains from data the publisher's online service returns, and brings them in through two story missions. Its switch, payload, conversion tables, the look it replays from Mordor's generator and Mordor's own captain document are described in The Nemesis Forge: imported captains.

Open questions

  • Whether Mordor's engine lays its hand-grip layers (Orc_L_Hand_Weapon / Orc_R_Hand_Weapon) over its body clips, as War plays its copy of them while fighting, and how War blends a layer over a clip's own finger keys.
  • Which of the two death bits of Mordor's FactionMember status word, bit 2 (IsDead) or bit 23 (IsMockDead), the Army screen shows as dead.
  • What Mordor's saved FactionMember floats +0x378 - +0x380 and the paired values +0x384 / +0x388 hold (a position and two morale-like stats are the candidates), and whether the 0x3B8 pool stride holds pool data beside each 0x3B0-byte member.
