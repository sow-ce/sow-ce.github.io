Shadow of War: Community Edition

A mod menu for Middle-earth: Shadow of War that works on every PC copy of the game: Steam, GOG and the Microsoft Store (Xbox app). Install it with the setup, then press TAB in game to open it.

Patch notes

Drop a mod on the game

SoW:CE installs and runs your Shadow of War mods by itself. Download a mod from Nexus, drop it on the game, and switch it on and off while you play. There are no default.archcfg lines to write and no other mod loader to install.

  1. Download the mod as it comes: a .zip, .7z or .rar, or a single .arch06 or .dll.
  2. Drag the file onto the game window while you play, or put it in the Mods\_inbox folder in the game's folder.
  3. SoW:CE unpacks it, checks every file before it writes one, and installs it. The mod gets a card in the menu's Mods tab.
  4. The card's switch turns the mod on and off while you play, with no restart.

Archives and packets come in switched on. A Mod Loader plugin comes in switched off, because it's code: switch it on when you're ready.

What kinds of mods work?

Archive mods (.arch06), packet mods, loose game files and Mod Loader plugins. A download that comes with FOMOD choices waits on its card until you pick them, and only what you pick is installed. Each card shows the mod's Nexus name and version and which files it replaces.

Two mods change the same file

Each card says which mods it clashes with, and the Conflicts filter in the Mods tab lists every pair of mods that share a file. From there you can put the one you want on top.

On the Microsoft Store copy

Drag a download from your Downloads folder or your desktop onto the game, and it installs the same way.

Wings and flight

Talion can wear wings that move with him, and fly on them: take off, climb, dive, hover and glide over Mordor, and fight from the air.

  1. Press TAB, open Talion, then Appearance, and pick a look under Wings: the fell beast's leathery wings, a wraith pair, or the feathered Seraph wings.
  2. Set Flight to On in the same place. The Dashboard's Wings switch does both in one click, with the Seraph wings.
  3. Double-tap Space to take off. W flies where the camera looks, Space climbs, Ctrl descends and Shift beats hard.
  4. Let go to hover, or to glide when you're moving fast. Come down onto the ground to land, or press L to let go and fall.

The wings stay folded until he fights, unless you set them to stay spread. Flying Mode (No-Clip), under Talion > Movement, is separate: while it's on, it flies him instead of the wings.

What can he do in the air?

Look at an enemy below and press the swoop key to stoop onto him and finish him. Light attack drops on an enemy from any height, the wing buffet slams both wings down on whoever is below, and Talon Strike can be aimed from the air. After a move, one Space press launches him straight back up. Tap A or D twice to barrel-roll, and hold W into a wall while flying slowly to grab it.

Trails, colors and finishes

Wing trails draw ribbons of light from the wingtips, in 12 styles with their own color, brightness, length and width. The Seraph wings come in 13 colorways and take any Captain Studio finish, and the Wraith look under Armour Shaders turns Talion's gear into a pale ghost to match them.

Can captains have wings?

Yes. The Captain Studio's Aura page gives an orc captain the fell beast's wings, folded until he fights.

The Outpost War

In the normal game, a warchief outpost shuts down for good once you finish its mission. SoW:CE turns the outposts into a war you fight in every region: cleared outposts come back, change hands and get new commanders, and your own captains can lead the fight for them. It's one switch, and it's off until you turn it on.

  1. Press TAB, open Areas, then The Outpost War, and tick The Outpost War.
  2. To bring back a cleared outpost, open Fine-tune each part and click Re-arm next to it under Warchief outposts, or Re-arm cleared for all of a region's. Its signal fires light again.
  3. Be in that outpost's region and let world turns pass. A turn passes when you finish a mission, die or advance the day from the Army screen. When an enemy captain takes the outpost, its mission shows on the map.
  4. To send one of your captains, click "Open the outpost war map", pick the outpost and send him. He joins the fight when the outpost's commander comes out, and killing the commander earns him levels.

The Outpost War changes your save: outposts, their missions and new captains are saved like anything else you do in game. Untick it and none of the outpost mod runs.

Mission cards

An outpost's own mission card, on the map and on its start screen, says who holds the outpost, what it gives and which of your captains you can send. Where an outpost has one of the game's own missions, that mission leads, and its last objective reads "Kill <name> to control the outpost!"

The outpost war map

"Open the outpost war map", under Areas > The Outpost War, shows a 3D board of each region with its outposts and who holds them. Click an outpost for its details and orders.

Lithlad's outposts

Under Fine-tune each part, "Outposts on the Lithlad army" makes Desolation of Mordor's five outposts captain seats in the campaign. An outpost with one of your captains in its seat is yours. If that seat empties, the enemy starts to retake it at the next world turn and holds it three turns later. "Outposts on the map", in the same place, colors each one's area by who holds it, in colors you can pick.

Shadow of Mordor content

SoW:CE can bring Shadow of Mordor's orcs into Shadow of War: the armor, helmets, hair and weapons Monolith made for the first game, its armor sets, colors and flairs, and its named captains. Nothing of either game is in the download. The Mordor Orc Library builds it on your PC, from your own copy of Shadow of Mordor.

  1. Install SoW:CE in the Steam copy of Shadow of War. Shadow of Mordor stays where it's installed, in any of your Steam libraries.
  2. Open SoW-CE Tools on your desktop, or the setup, and turn on Shadow of Mordor content under Play with SoW:CE. The setup adds the Mordor Orc Library and opens it.
  3. Close the game, then click Convert and install. It finds Shadow of Mordor in your Steam libraries by itself; if it can't, click Choose folder under Shadow of Mordor and pick the folder it's installed in. The first run takes several minutes and about 2.5 GB of free space.
  4. Start the game with SoW:CE. On the Army screen, right-click a captain: the pieces are under Armor & body, marked [Mordor].

The pieces stay on your PC, and other players don't see them online. A captain wearing a Mordor piece shows that slot empty while the content is off.

What comes over?

More than 250 armor, helmet, hair and accessory pieces, the Black Captains' armor included, and Shadow of Mordor's own armor sets under Full set's "Looks from: Shadow of Mordor". A captain can carry one of its weapons, shields or bows, wear its flairs and take its color setups. The Captain Studio's Nemesis page brings Shadow of Mordor's named captains into your army, with their Mordor names in all ten of the game's languages.

Turning it off

Switch Shadow of Mordor content off in SoW-CE Tools to take it out of the game and keep its files, so it comes back in a moment. Remove it there to free the space as well. The same converted pack also holds the outposts' face-off and ambush and the Custom Fights prompts, so those go with it.

Does it work on GOG or the Microsoft Store?

Not yet. The converted pack works with the Steam copy of Shadow of War only, so the switch only shows there.

What this mod menu includes

Everything here is in the current version and has been tested in game. It's grouped the same way as the menu's tabs, and each entry shows where to find it.

    How to install

    1. Open the download page, read the risks, tick the checklist and download the setup, .
    2. Close the game, then run the setup. It finds your Steam or GOG install on its own. For the Microsoft Store copy, click Microsoft Store at the bottom of the setup instead.
    3. Click Install, start the game the way your copy needs (just below), and press TAB in game.

    Starting the game with SoW:CE

    • Steam on Windows: the Shadow of War (SoW-CE) shortcut on your desktop, or Play with SoW:CE in the setup. Steam's own Play button starts the game without the mod menu, unless you paste the setup's Steam launch option into Steam once (right-click Shadow of War > Properties > General > Launch Options).
    • GOG on Windows: the same Shadow of War (SoW-CE) shortcut, or Play with SoW:CE in the setup. GOG Galaxy's Play button always starts the game without the mod menu.
    • Microsoft Store (Xbox app): the Shadow of War (SoW-CE, Microsoft Store) shortcut, or Play with SoW:CE in the setup's Microsoft Store window. Shadow of War in the Start menu or the Xbox app starts the game without the mod menu. Start the game once by itself before you install, so Windows makes its data folder.
    • Linux or Steam Deck: click Copy Steam launch option in the setup, paste it into Shadow of War's Launch Options in Steam once, then use Steam's Play button. This is still experimental, see Troubleshooting.

    New builds offer themselves when you start the game, and SoW-CE Tools on your desktop handles repairs, reports and uninstalling. Do a clean remove before any Windows, driver or game update.

    You'll need

    • A legitimate copy of Middle-earth: Shadow of War on Steam or GOG, game version 1.21 (on GOG, the Definitive Edition), or the Microsoft Store copy, app version 1.0.28.2
    • All of the game's DLC, downloaded and installed
    • Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit). Linux and the Steam Deck, through Proton, are experimental.
    • The Visual C++ x64 runtime (the setup tells you if it's missing)
    • On Windows N or KN editions, the Media Feature Pack

    FAQ

    Something not working? Go to Troubleshooting.

    Which version of the game does it work with?

    The Steam version or the GOG version (Definitive Edition), game version 1.21, or the Microsoft Store copy (app version 1.0.28.2), on 64-bit Windows 10 or 11. Other versions won't work. The setup checks your game before it changes anything. On Linux and the Steam Deck, through Proton, it's an experiment: see Troubleshooting.

    I moved from Steam to GOG. Can I keep my progress?

    Yes. The GOG copy can't read Steam's saves as they are, so the setup copies them over for you. Close the game, open the setup or SoW-CE Tools with your GOG copy picked, and click Copy Steam saves under Play with SoW:CE (it's in Saves too). Story, Blade of Galadriel and Desolation of Mordor all come across. Your Steam saves are only read, and any GOG saves it would replace are backed up first. If GOG Galaxy's cloud saves are on for Shadow of War, turn them off first, or Galaxy may put its old copy back.

    I moved from GOG to Steam. Can I keep my progress?

    Yes. Install SoW:CE into your Steam copy first, then close the game, open the setup with your Steam copy picked, and click Copy GOG saves to Steam in Saves. Story, Blade of Galadriel and Desolation of Mordor all come across. Then start the game with SoW:CE (the desktop shortcut or Play with SoW:CE): before the game reads your save, SoW:CE hands it to Steam itself. Steam reads its saves through its own records rather than straight from the folder, so without that step the game would start a new game. Your GOG saves are only read, and the Steam saves it replaces are backed up first. The same goes for copying Microsoft Store saves to Steam.

    I play the Microsoft Store (Xbox app) copy

    Start the game once by itself, close it, then run the setup and click Microsoft Store at the bottom. Install puts SoW:CE into the Store copy's own data folder (Windows owns the game folder itself) and makes a Shadow of War (SoW-CE, Microsoft Store) shortcut. On my Store copy the game couldn't load or keep a save at all; with SoW:CE it does. To move progress between the Store copy and a Steam or GOG copy, open Saves in the setup and click Microsoft Store: it copies either way, checks every file and backs up what it replaces. A copy into the Store copy is applied the next time you start the Store copy with SoW:CE. New builds don't install themselves on the Store copy: run the new setup and click Update in its Microsoft Store window.

    How do I open the menu?

    Press TAB or F9 in game. Inside the menu, Ctrl+F jumps to the main features.

    Will it mess with my save?

    Most of it only lasts while the game is running. Things you change in the world, like moving a captain, bringing one back or re-arming an outpost, get saved the same as anything else you do in game. Your saves are also backed up when you start the game with SoW:CE, and the setup can restore any of those backups.

    Why do my mods turn off when I restart?

    SoW:CE's own features in the menu start off at every launch on purpose, so a bad setting can't follow you into the next one. If you want them to stay on, turn on "Remember mods between launches" under Settings > Mods & Startup. Mods you install through the Mods tab are different: their switches are kept between launches.

    How do I bring cleared outposts back?

    Tick The Outpost War under Areas in the menu, then open Fine-tune each part and click Re-arm under Warchief outposts. The steps are under The Outpost War.

    I re-armed an outpost. Where's its mission?

    It needs a world turn and an enemy captain to take the outpost, and it only shows while you're in that region. Captains are busy with other things for three turns after you arrive, so give it a few. With The Outpost War on, the nemesis system keeps giving the enemy's outposts their commanders. In Lithlad, an outpost you hold gets no warchief mission until the enemy takes it back, which is the game's own rule.

    Where did the outpost shields go?

    The shields drawn over the world map are gone. The outposts' missions, cards and banners now come with The Outpost War, which is off until you tick it under Areas. To keep it on after a restart, also turn on "Remember mods between launches".

    How do I get the Shadow of Mordor content?

    You need your own copy of Shadow of Mordor, installed wherever Steam put it, and SoW:CE in the Steam copy of Shadow of War. Turn on Shadow of Mordor content in the setup, under Play with SoW:CE, and convert it in the Mordor Orc Library that opens. The steps are under Shadow of Mordor content. If your game updated itself to this build, download the new setup once and run it: the switch comes with the setup.

    How do I install a mod from Nexus?

    Download it as it comes, then drag the file onto the game window while you play, or put it in the Mods\_inbox folder in the game's folder. SoW:CE unpacks it, checks every file and installs it, and the mod gets a card in the menu's Mods tab with a switch that turns it on and off while you play. The steps are under Drop a mod on the game.

    Do I still need archcfg lines, Packet Loader or Mod Loader?

    No. SoW:CE mounts archives, serves packets and runs Mod Loader plugins by itself. If another loader is still installed, SoW:CE leaves its mods to it so nothing loads twice, and the Mods tab says so. An archive that a line in default.archcfg still mounts is left to that line: take the line out, and its switch in the Mods tab works live.

    How do I update?

    You don't have to download anything. When a new build is out, SoW:CE asks as the game starts and fetches only what changed, usually a few MB. Say no and it asks again next time. The Microsoft Store copy is the exception: there, run the new setup and click Update in its Microsoft Store window. Shadow of Mordor content is the other one: its switch comes with the setup, so download the new setup once to get it.

    How do I uninstall?

    Open SoW-CE Tools on your desktop and click Uninstall at the bottom of the window.

    Do I need to remove it before Windows or game updates?

    Yes. Do a clean remove before any Windows, graphics driver or game update, so nothing of SoW:CE's gets in the way of the game's DRM. The steps are on the download page.

    Troubleshooting

    Start with Check my install. Open SoW-CE Tools on your desktop and click it: it looks over the game, SoW:CE's files and your PC, changes nothing, and tells you what to do about anything it finds.

    I don't have SoW-CE Tools on my desktop

    You're on an older setup. Download the new one and install it over the top. New builds offer themselves from then on.

    There's a blank icon called "Shadow of War (SoW" on my desktop

    That was my mistake: older setups made a broken shortcut that can't start anything. Install the new setup and it's replaced with a working one called Shadow of War (SoW-CE). You can also just delete the blank one.

    Windows says the setup might be unsafe

    The setup isn't code-signed, so SmartScreen warns about it. Only download it from this site. If you want, check its SHA-256 against the one in How to install, then click More info and Run anyway.

    My antivirus removed something

    New tools that aren't code-signed often get flagged. Add the game's x64\sowframework folder to your antivirus's exceptions, then click Check my install and press Repair.

    The setup can't find my game

    Click Change folder and pick the folder with ShadowOfWar.exe in it. To find it, right-click Shadow of War in Steam, choose Manage, then Browse local files, and open the x64 folder. On GOG, open the game's settings menu in GOG Galaxy and choose Manage installation, then Show folder. If you have both copies, the setup has a link to switch between them, next to Change folder.

    TAB doesn't open the menu

    You probably started the game from Steam or GOG Galaxy, which start it without the mod menu. Use the Shadow of War (SoW-CE) shortcut or Play with SoW:CE instead. If you'd rather use Steam's Play button, the setup can copy a launch option for you to paste into Steam once. GOG Galaxy has no such option, so on GOG use the shortcut.

    I have both the Steam and the GOG copy

    The setup works on one copy at a time. Next to Change folder it has a link to switch to the other copy, so install into each copy you want to play with SoW:CE. Each copy keeps its own saves; to bring your progress from one copy to the other, see "I moved from Steam to GOG" and "I moved from GOG to Steam" in the FAQ.

    The game won't start, or it keeps crashing

    In SoW-CE Tools, click Fresh config. The next start uses the defaults with every mod off, so you find out whether a saved setting is the cause. Nothing is deleted, and Restore brings your settings back. If that doesn't help, hold Shift while the game starts to play without SoW:CE, and send me a report.

    'NoneType' object has no attribute 'lower', or error 740

    Both are fixed. You've got an older setup, so download the new one.

    The game asks to be activated after a Windows update

    Do a clean remove, get the game running by itself, then reinstall. The steps are on the download page.

    No update shows up

    SoW:CE looks for a new build when you start the game with the shortcut, Play with SoW:CE or the Steam launch option. Steam's and GOG Galaxy's plain Play buttons skip it, and with no connection the game just starts.

    A new build gave me trouble

    Hold Ctrl while starting SoW:CE to go back one build. That build isn't offered again, but the next new one is.

    I'm on Linux or a Steam Deck

    SoW:CE is made for Windows, so on Linux it's an experiment, and I'd love to hear how it goes. Run the setup through Proton: in Steam, choose Add a Non-Steam Game, pick the setup, force a Proton version under its Properties > Compatibility, and start it. It should find your game and your saves by itself. If it doesn't, click Change folder: it shows hidden folders like .steam, and you can also paste the game folder's path from your file manager. Your Steam folder's name starts with a dot, so Linux hides it (Ctrl+H shows it in most file managers), but don't rename it, Steam needs that name. Then click Copy Steam launch option, paste it into Shadow of War's Launch Options in Steam, and start the game from Steam. Updates can't install themselves on Linux yet, so get new builds from this site. If the game doesn't open, x64/sowframework/logs/boot_launcher.txt says where SoW:CE's launcher stopped: send it to me with your report.

    Something I start from the menu doesn't happen

    The game pauses while it's in the background, so anything you start from the menu waits until you're back in the game.

    The game froze

    If you started it with SoW:CE, its launcher notices when the game stops responding and writes down where it was stuck, in x64\sowframework\logs\framework\hangs. Send that file with your report. If a plugin was loading when a start went wrong, SoW:CE leaves it out of the next start, and its card in the Mods tab says so: switch it back on when you're ready.

    None of that helped

    In SoW-CE Tools, click Report a problem. It saves a zip on your desktop with your logs and what Check my install found.
