Tools

Standalone programs I've made for Shadow of War. They run next to the game instead of inside it, so there's nothing to install. Unzip one, start the game, then run it.

Looking for SoW-CE Tools, the app the setup puts on your desktop? That one comes with the main download.

  • Haedir Towers

    Purifies the Haedirs so you don't have to climb them again on every new save. Do all 15 at once or one region at a time, and turn them back to Sauron's whenever you like.
