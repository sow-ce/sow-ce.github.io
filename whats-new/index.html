What's new in SoW:CE

The build of 27 November 2026

This build brings four big additions: mods you install by dropping them on the game, wings Talion can fly on, The Outpost War, and Shadow of Mordor's orcs for your captains. Here's how to use each one.

  • Drop a mod on the game
  • Wings and flight
  • The Outpost War
  • Shadow of Mordor content

Everything else that changed is in the patch notes. New to SoW:CE? Download the mod menu.

Drop a mod on the game

SoW:CE installs and runs your Shadow of War mods by itself. Download a mod from Nexus, drop it on the game, and switch it on and off while you play. There are no default.archcfg lines to write and no other mod loader to install.

  1. Download the mod as it comes: a .zip, .7z or .rar, or a single .arch06 or .dll.
  2. Drag the file onto the game window while you play, or put it in the Mods\_inbox folder in the game's folder.
  3. SoW:CE unpacks it, checks every file before it writes one, and installs it. The mod gets a card in the menu's Mods tab.
  4. The card's switch turns the mod on and off while you play, with no restart.

Archives and packets come in switched on. A Mod Loader plugin comes in switched off, because it's code: switch it on when you're ready.

What kinds of mods work?

Archive mods (.arch06), packet mods, loose game files and Mod Loader plugins. A download that comes with FOMOD choices waits on its card until you pick them, and only what you pick is installed. Each card shows the mod's Nexus name and version and which files it replaces.

Two mods change the same file

Each card says which mods it clashes with, and the Conflicts filter in the Mods tab lists every pair of mods that share a file. From there you can put the one you want on top.

On the Microsoft Store copy

Drag a download from your Downloads folder or your desktop onto the game, and it installs the same way.

Wings and flight

Talion can wear wings that move with him, and fly on them: take off, climb, dive, hover and glide over Mordor, and fight from the air.

  1. Press TAB, open Talion, then Appearance, and pick a look under Wings: the fell beast's leathery wings, a wraith pair, or the feathered Seraph wings.
  2. Set Flight to On in the same place. The Dashboard's Wings switch does both in one click, with the Seraph wings.
  3. Double-tap Space to take off. W flies where the camera looks, Space climbs, Ctrl descends and Shift beats hard.
  4. Let go to hover, or to glide when you're moving fast. Come down onto the ground to land, or press L to let go and fall.

The wings stay folded until he fights, unless you set them to stay spread. Flying Mode (No-Clip), under Talion > Movement, is separate: while it's on, it flies him instead of the wings.

What can he do in the air?

Look at an enemy below and press the swoop key to stoop onto him and finish him. Light attack drops on an enemy from any height, the wing buffet slams both wings down on whoever is below, and Talon Strike can be aimed from the air. After a move, one Space press launches him straight back up. Tap A or D twice to barrel-roll, and hold W into a wall while flying slowly to grab it.

Trails, colors and finishes

Wing trails draw ribbons of light from the wingtips, in 12 styles with their own color, brightness, length and width. The Seraph wings come in 13 colorways and take any Captain Studio finish, and the Wraith look under Armour Shaders turns Talion's gear into a pale ghost to match them.

Can captains have wings?

Yes. The Captain Studio's Aura page gives an orc captain the fell beast's wings, folded until he fights.

The Outpost War

In the normal game, a warchief outpost shuts down for good once you finish its mission. SoW:CE turns the outposts into a war you fight in every region: cleared outposts come back, change hands and get new commanders, and your own captains can lead the fight for them. It's one switch, and it's off until you turn it on.

  1. Press TAB, open Areas, then The Outpost War, and tick The Outpost War.
  2. To bring back a cleared outpost, open Fine-tune each part and click Re-arm next to it under Warchief outposts, or Re-arm cleared for all of a region's. Its signal fires light again.
  3. Be in that outpost's region and let world turns pass. A turn passes when you finish a mission, die or advance the day from the Army screen. When an enemy captain takes the outpost, its mission shows on the map.
  4. To send one of your captains, click "Open the outpost war map", pick the outpost and send him. He joins the fight when the outpost's commander comes out, and killing the commander earns him levels.

The Outpost War changes your save: outposts, their missions and new captains are saved like anything else you do in game. Untick it and none of the outpost mod runs.

Mission cards

An outpost's own mission card, on the map and on its start screen, says who holds the outpost, what it gives and which of your captains you can send. Where an outpost has one of the game's own missions, that mission leads, and its last objective reads "Kill <name> to control the outpost!"

The outpost war map

"Open the outpost war map", under Areas > The Outpost War, shows a 3D board of each region with its outposts and who holds them. Click an outpost for its details and orders.

Lithlad's outposts

Under Fine-tune each part, "Outposts on the Lithlad army" makes Desolation of Mordor's five outposts captain seats in the campaign. An outpost with one of your captains in its seat is yours. If that seat empties, the enemy starts to retake it at the next world turn and holds it three turns later. "Outposts on the map", in the same place, colors each one's area by who holds it, in colors you can pick.

Shadow of Mordor content

SoW:CE can bring Shadow of Mordor's orcs into Shadow of War: the armor, helmets, hair and weapons Monolith made for the first game, its armor sets, colors and flairs, and its named captains. Nothing of either game is in the download. The Mordor Orc Library builds it on your PC, from your own copy of Shadow of Mordor.

  1. Install SoW:CE in the Steam copy of Shadow of War. Shadow of Mordor stays where it's installed, in any of your Steam libraries.
  2. Open SoW-CE Tools on your desktop, or the setup, and turn on Shadow of Mordor content under Play with SoW:CE. The setup adds the Mordor Orc Library and opens it.
  3. Close the game, then click Convert and install. It finds Shadow of Mordor in your Steam libraries by itself; if it can't, click Choose folder under Shadow of Mordor and pick the folder it's installed in. The first run takes several minutes and about 2.5 GB of free space.
  4. Start the game with SoW:CE. On the Army screen, right-click a captain: the pieces are under Armor & body, marked [Mordor].

The pieces stay on your PC, and other players don't see them online. A captain wearing a Mordor piece shows that slot empty while the content is off.

What comes over?

More than 250 armor, helmet, hair and accessory pieces, the Black Captains' armor included, and Shadow of Mordor's own armor sets under Full set's "Looks from: Shadow of Mordor". A captain can carry one of its weapons, shields or bows, wear its flairs and take its color setups. The Captain Studio's Nemesis page brings Shadow of Mordor's named captains into your army, with their Mordor names in all ten of the game's languages.

Turning it off

Switch Shadow of Mordor content off in SoW-CE Tools to take it out of the game and keep its files, so it comes back in a moment. Remove it there to free the space as well. The same converted pack also holds the outposts' face-off and ambush and the Custom Fights prompts, so those go with it.

Does it work on GOG or the Microsoft Store?

Not yet. The converted pack works with the Steam copy of Shadow of War only, so the switch only shows there.
